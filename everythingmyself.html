Fic:

The first time it happened, it wasn’t planned.

Well. Neither were the subsequent times, but the first time was truly a spur of the moment thing.

They’ve known each other for a while at that point, Claire introducing them a few years earlier, and both just happened to be at the same follow-up meeting about the whole mess that happened in Italy with the ships.

While the anti-bioterror part of USSTRATCOM was not yet fully done becoming the DSO, they still felt the need to supervise all proceedings regarding the defunct FBC, which is where Leon came in; drawing the short straw on being one of the babysitters for this particular meeting.

Leon and Claire were still in the middle of their falling out over that other, non FBC related, government conspiracy, but that didn’t really stop him and Chris from sticking to each other basically the whole time: whatever distance Leon might have wanted from the whole Redfield bloodline taking a backseat to trying to survive the drivel of higher ups about a nearly 2-year-old incident that everyone involved thought of as done and dusted.

They got to talking and then things simply… aligned.

In their small talk during the meeting, they found out they were both relatively free after, so as soon as they could they ditched the place, going back to Chris’ hotel to catch up

Nothing nefarious.

When they talked about it later they both sincerely agreed they just wanted to take a break from prying eyes and chat in peace with no sex-related ulterior motive.

They were talking, really. Talking, laughing, catching up. Like friends do. And through that very normal friendly chatter it very quickly became apparent they were both very close to snapping: Leon drowning in at least 8 layers of government coverup and Chris still having Jill’s presumed death at Wesker’s hands fresh in his mind.

And well… somehow (don’t ask Chris how) they started maybe… making out on the hotel couch a little… Which eventually moved to the bed and to a lot more than making out.

And then in the morning they had a heart to heart in which they mutually agreed they really needed that and that it didn’t change anything between them. Which. To be fair. They also stopped several times during both the make out on the couch and then the rest of the night to establish and then confirm these two specific facts. So not like there was much doubt about it in the morning.

They were adults. They could have casual sex to relieve pent up emotions brought on by their high-stakes high-stress jobs if they wanted to.

And honest to god it was supposed to be a one-time thing. A ‘we both really needed the outlet and were comfortable enough with each other to do it, but Claire (or anyone really) can never find out because they would make it into a whole thing’ thing.

But well… It happened again. And again. And after the fifth time they sat down and actually talked about it. And once again they settled mutually on: high stress job, not a lot of healthy coping mechanisms, they trust each other a lot and well… admittedly the sex was really good, so why not have a casual thing with each other?

It didn’t happen every time they met up. Far from it. But sometimes during their catch ups one would say something along the lines of “Man, I’ve been really stressed recently.” with a meaningful look, and the other almost always responded with “Yea, sure, I’m down. You got a place?” and then they would be each other’s for the night.

They never really had problems with this arrangement. The formula was so simple it became just another part of Chris’ life: meet up, have amazing emotions-filled sex, talk about what was bothering them and also how the sex was, cuddle to fight off the need for intimate touch of the not sexual kind and then go their separate ways in the morning.

Sure, okay, Chris maybe has been daydreaming about waking up with a kiss along with the ‘Hey let go you huge teddy bear I gotta piss’ for a few years now, but who in their line of work hasn’t had daydreams of a peaceful life of stability. It didn’t mean anything. And well even if it did, then it wasn’t like Chris was about to try and pursue anything anyway.

Come on. Leon? Beautiful, breathtaking, funny and charming Leon? Wanting anything more permanent with him?

Oh, Chris could only dream.

Which to be fair he did. A lot. And perhaps sometimes when he was alone and had trouble sleeping due to being pent up, he would reach into his pants as well as his memories and maybe… indulge a little to the thought of Leon being coiled up with pleasure and whimpering just for him. But it was fine. Chris was content with their dynamic as it was and didn’t feel the need to change it.

In any case, they never had a problem with what they had going on. Both interpersonally and in the hiding-it-from-other-people department.

Well at least until the night after Alcatraz.

Okay not the night after. While that was when him and Leon fell into yet another hotel bed together (even getting rather unusually passionate as for once the sex was riding the high of a successful mission, not dealing with unsaid and otherwise undealt-with emotions), it was 2 days after that night, when Chris finally emerged from his makeshift office with all paperwork and reports about the incident dealt with, that he and Claire went to get lunch together, making use of the opportunity of being in the same country for once to have a little sibling time that didn’t involve being infected with a virus and then fighting a bioweapon.

A lunch for which he made one crucial error.

He wore a normal shirt.

See, the little cubicle area that served as Chris’ office for the time being was very heavily air conditioned, and therefore Chris, having known that he would spend an uncomfortable amount of time there writing his report, decided to wear a turtleneck as he spent 20 hours of pain and misery behind a keyboard there.

Those 20 hours then led to another step down the unfortunate path that would lead him where he was now, as once he got home, he took a shower on the finest of sleep deprived autopilots, not sparing even a single glance at himself in the bathroom mirror, which led him down the path of no return as he put on whatever t-shirt seemed the cleanest to his meetup with Claire without a second thought.

All was well as they ordered drinks and food and talked about whatever (their lives, how much of a loser that Dylan guy was, etcetera).

But then Chris rolled his shoulders absentmindedly and watched with a subconscious pang of dread as Claire’s eyes narrowed and back straightened.

“What?” he asked.

“That’s a hickey.” Not a question. A statement.

“Huh?” his own eyes widened in horror as he subconsciously put his hand to the spot on his lower neck that did admittedly still feel a bit sore.

Fuck, they were usually really careful about not leaving marks anywhere visible, but now that he spared it a mortified thought, Leon was just so breathtaking as he sucked and nibbled at his shoulders and neck; like he just wanted to devour Chris whole.

And man, Chris was very much on board with being devoured in that moment.

He felt his cheeks heat up at the recollection. Another mistake, Claire no doubt catching even the smallest change to his complexion like a hawk.

She looked equal parts mad, scandalized and disbelieving, “Christopher Thomas Redfield did you get laid? In the barely two days we haven’t seen each other in?”

Yikes, not the fake full name with a complimentary fake middle name and a completely true accusation as the cherry on top. He would not be getting out of this one easily. But he had to try, didn’t he?

“Um well… maybe?” he was probably wearing the dumbest expression known to man. With his luck most likely with a generous dash of being hopelessly lovestruck.

“Who is it.” she narrowed her eyes further, expression fully shifting to planning a murder or at least a very heavily worded shovel talk.

“Oh um… you don’t… know… them.” Keep it ambiguous.

She scoffed and rolled her eyes, “Puh-lease.” she paused, “Is he from work? Is it D.C.? I thought he was away. And also still not over the divorce… Is it that restaurant owner you talk about sometimes- no, you said he’s married, you wouldn’t do that. Oh! That attendant guy from the BSAA shooting range, I saw him making eyes at you, but no, you haven’t mentioned him before, also he’s a little young for you. So who is it. And before you say I don’t know him we both know that is a lie, you don’t do one night stands. You have too much of a big heart for that, no, you’d have to know the guy before sleeping with him…” she finished her small rant, putting her hands together and scanning him with her eyes, like she could somehow read who he slept with from his expression alone.

Worst of all Chris feared she could.

He tactically diverted the topic a little, “That’s a bit presumptuous don’t you think? Just naming guys?” They never talked about his sexuality (mostly because his sex life was about as dry as the Sahara Desert), how the hell did she go straight to only guessing men?

She raised a very sceptical, very done-with-him eyebrow, “Was it a woman?”

“…No.”

“And you don’t know any people who are neither, so there you go.”

Chris looked away sheepishly. She did have a point, but it still didn’t feel all too great to be read like a book in such a way.

She had multiple points, actually. Because Claire was right. Chris wasn’t the type to sleep with a stranger.

That’s why he was sleeping with his best friend.

“So spill, who captured my big brother’s even bigger heart and how come I’m only hearing about this relationship now and not when you got together.”

‘Because he’s also your best friend and the first time we slept together you two were not on speaking terms.’ Did not feel like the correct answer.

Well lying would get him nowhere with how clearly Claire was reading him. Maybe a half truth about the nature of their relationship would do?

“Oh it’s… not really. Like that. It’s just to blow off steam, get rid of excess pressure, that sort of thing.” He started drawing circles into the tacky restaurant tablecloth with his finger.

Claire’s expression went blank, her arms falling on the table.

What did that mean???

Her eyes flitted across his no doubt still red face, before she leaned back, eyebrows lifting up, rest of her expression still weirdly blank.

“And that’s… all that it is for you?”

Chris looked away again. Fuck she had him in a corner.

He sighed, “I am… happy with our current dynamic.” That was diplomatic enough, right?

“That’s not a yes, Chris.”

He knew that.

“It’s not, but it is true.”

She crossed her arms, “How long has this ‘dynamic’ been going on?”

“A while.”

“Chris.” She warned.

He looked away, “A while.” A number of years too uncomfortably close to having double digits for him to admit it this easily.

“So a year?”

His expression pinched uncomfortably without his permission.

“Years?!” her eyebrows shot up slightly before she sighed, putting her head in one of her hands, mumbling, “Goddammit you two.” under her breath.

“Mhm.” Chris hummed awkwardly, still looking away.

Wait.

‘You two’?

He didn’t have much time to dwell on the wording as Claire reached for her phone, quickly tapping something on it before putting it to her ear, dialling someone.

“Uuh…” said Chris intelligently, looking at his sister with confusion, who just held up her hand, telling him to wait.

The other person picked up, their voice unintelligible over the low rustle of the restaurant.

Claire didn’t let them speak for long, “Hey, I’ve just been wondering. So remember that talk we had the other week? …Yea the one about you having a massive crush on Chris.”

Huh?

“Yea, listen, why didn’t it come up during that entire hours long thing that you have been sleeping together for actual years.”

Oh god she’s accusing some poor guy who apparently has a crush on him. Chris tried to reach out or otherwise gesture for her to stop, but she just shot him a rather unimpressed look.

“…No he hasn’t. But you see he showed up with a hickey today, y’know, after he didn’t have it the last time we saw each other two days ago? During which there was a very small amount of people he has seen in that very limited time? And guess what he said about it. Would you hazard a guess as to what he said about it? That’s right, that it was just to ‘blow off steam’. Now. Isn’t it funny, Leon, that you gave me that exact response when caught in that exact situation a year ago? Which I now realise was just after you two had that mission together. I think it’s rather hilarious.”

Leon?! She was talking to Leon? No way, right? And oh god a year ago, that was when Arias happened. Seeing Leon so depressed maybe made Chris a little more… passionate than normal when they happened to sleep together after and honestly he doesn’t remember, but well he probably left behind a few marks above the collar that time…

He was just so angry at how the world treated Leon and was trying to pour all his hope and love into their encounter; to selfishly show Leon that he had people that wanted him, that cared about him.

“Massive crush?” he found himself saying. Dumbfounded.

The other line went dead, whereas before Leon could be heard trying to justify himself/probably cover their asses.

Then there was a very small mumble from the speaker.

“Yes.”

Another one.

“Yes.”

One with a questioning tone.

“Sure.”

And then Chris was being handed the phone by Claire who mouthed, ‘go for it tiger’ at him with an expression that was somehow both completely done with him and yet earnestly encouraging.

“Um. Hi. Leon.”, he put his head in his free hand, hiding away from Claire’s eyes. This conversation will be hard enough as is.

“Hey Chris.” Well at least he sounded as awkward as Chris did.

“You left one above the collar, Leon.”

“Got that part, yea.”

“We should probably. Talk about this in person.”

Leon sighed, he sounded… hurt, “Listen, I’m a big boy, I can handle rejection over the phone.”

Oh, fuck he thought he was being rejected. Right, stupid, there was no talk of Chris’ side of the crush, “Who said anything about rejection?”

“Goes without saying, doesn’t it?” and fuck Chris hated that tone of voice. It was the same one he had in Colorado. One Chris hoped he would never use again.

God, that selfish need to make Leon feel loved took over him again, not that it ever really left, “No, it doesn’t.”

The next pause felt like it stretched on forever.

“Wait really? Seriously?” he sounded incredulous, but hopeful.

“Yea. Seriously.”

“Then should we maybe… make it a date?”

Chris felt warmth flood his entire chest, yes, yes, YES! A date where he could hold Leon’s hand just because, followed by a night where they would cuddle and a morning where Chris would wake Leon up with a kiss and breakfast in bed!

…Okay his heart was getting a bit ahead of his brain… thought all that would be very nice.

He cleared his throat, “Sure. I’d… I’d like that a lot. I’m free for the rest of the week, how about you?”

“Friday at 6. I’ll text you the address.”

Chris felt giddy, he couldn’t help but grin.

“Sounds great!” he finally straightened up, getting an eyeful of Claire who wore a devastatingly smug smirk, “I-I mean. Good. Um. See you there… I’mgivingthephonebacktoClairenowbye.”

He basically threw the phone back at her.

Claire took it and put it to her ear smoothly, “I’ll have words with you later, Kennedy, for now I’d like to finish my lunch… Yep… Die in a fire, love you, buh-bye.” And hung up.

She shook her head with a smile, “You’re both idiots.”

“Yeah, we are.”

Two idiots that were going on a date.

Chris couldn’t wait for Friday.
