Blink twice if you're being held hostage, Li

“I said no.”

“Have it your way.”

With that the swordsman walked back to his uncle. It truly was a shame; Li was prime Freedom Fighter material. But taking care of the only family you have left being a number one priority is something Jet could respect.

Speaking of Li's uncle, he is just the kind of person the scarred swordsman needed right now, at least in Jet's not very humble opinion. A kind and wise positive influence, who-

...who had “the coldest tea in Ba Sing Se” just a moment ago.

A firebender.

Li turned back around for just a moment, catching sight of the moment the realisation hit Jet’s expression. His one functional eye narrowed, a grim reminder of just what someone of his “uncle’s” kind could do.

Jet looked away just as a thought solidified in his head:

He needed to get Li away from that ashmaker.

 

 

“Jet this is ridiculous, all this ‘cause of one cup of hot tea?” Smellerbee complained not for the first time since Jet dragged them all out that morning. It was around evening now, yet another day wasted instead of trying to find a sustainable job.

Their merry band of misfits was standing before a dingy looking tea shop, the same one they stalked the previous few days, the one where the supposed “evil firebender” and his “hostage” grumpy nephew currently resided.

“We can’t just leave him Bee, you know that.” His tone turned cold “Plus a scar that big? It had to come from somewhere and now we have a prime suspect.” He turned to his other companion “You agree with me, right Longshot?”

The archer stared, then shook his head slightly in the negative. A no, but not exactly enthusiastic.

“See Longshot sees my point!”

“So do I! But this is stupid Jet, you have no evidence. So far we’ve just been stalking two refugees who want a fresh start, which, let me remind you, is what WE came to do here!”

“We can get evidence! That’s why we’re here, okay? We get the evidence the old guy is holding Li hostage and then report him to the Dai Li.”

Smellerbee shook her head, putting it in her hands with a frustrated groan. Stupid, pig-bull headed, stubborn Jet. No way the two of them were convincing him to give up.

Jet at least had the decency to look somewhat ashamed to be doing this to them “We’re just customers, this is a teashop right? We will drink some tea, we will gather intel, we’ll prove he’s Fire nation. Flawless plan.”

Smellerbee groaned louder.

 

 

“Uncle, the people from the ferry are here.” Came Zuko’s gritted out whisper “I told you the leader saw you firebending that stupid cup of tea, now they’re here to blackmail us.” “Or maybe they’re here to enjoy a nice cup of tea! Word gets around quick in places like this. From what I’ve heard we’ve gotten quite popular.” Iroh replied with a grin “Go on nephew, take their order, it’s rude to keep the customer waiting.”

It spoke volumes of the prince’s training that no stray sparks escaped his mouth along with the frustrated groan he let out as he stormed out of the backroom kitchen. Not that the prince would think himself any sort of skillful, but his uncle’s smile widened further. He has trained him well.

 

 

“Pao’s Teashop, what do you want.”

The leader began “Hey Li, say, how’s Ba Sing Se been so far?” For some reason, that earned him a stink eye from one of his companions and a slightly raised eyebrow from the other one. The one giving the stink eye, oh spirits, what was her name, something with small bees? Zuko now wished he didn’t discard their names the moment he thought they would never see each other again. And the mute one was related to archery somehow-

“We’ll take 2 specials and a chun mee.” Right. Teashop. Server. His job. No time to ponder the names of his customers/probable near-future blackmailers.

“Okay.” Zuko turns around, but his sleeve is caught by the wheat-mouth. “Anyway, just wanted to say it’s nice that Ba Sing Se is such a secure city, I mean even the Dragon of the west couldn’t get through, plus you have the Dai Li” ah, there’s the blackmail, Zuko thought rather calmly. Then the much more dangerous implications about the Dragon of the west mention crawled into his mind.

They didn’t just know they were firebenders, they knew who they were.

 

 

Li’s face turned a few shades paler, making his scar sit in much starker contrast with the rest of his face. His expression also became alarmed almost afraid, though he hid it under his perpetual scowl quite admirably. Jet was right and it broke his heart, he was so scared of his “uncle” that he felt he couldn’t go to the Dai Li for help. Just how good of a firebender was this man? Li was a prodigious swordsman, no way just some run of the mill ashmaker could hold him in such a tight grip. Maybe he has blackmail on him, or he’s holding his real family hosta-

His thoughts were interrupted with a sharp elbow to his ribs. “Ouch! The hell Bee?” “What are you doing!” the girl whisper-yelled in Jet’s ear, then straightened and addressed the now released teaboy. “So sorry about him, just get us the teas. We heard they’re really good! Right guys?” She cast a scornful look in Jet’s direction. Longshot, the traitor, sagely nodded.

Jet readjusted the piece of straw in his mouth and crossed his arms “Yeah, right, good tea.”

Li seemed to calm a little bit, his eyes flitting across all of them, before he nodded and disappeared into the back room again.

“Jet you idiot, what part of low-profile, it’s-just-a-friendly-visit didn’t you understand?! You basically asked him to report his uncle.”

“Ha! So you do agree he’s trouble.”

“No, I’m saying they’re both clearly hiding something, and we should respect that. They’ve come to get new lives here, just like us. AND just like us, their past might get them in trouble with the Dai Li, so: Li went whiter than a snowstorm at the mention of them. Big deal. Yeah, a scar like that doesn’t come from nowhere Jet, but have you considered the skills that he has with a sword also don’t come from nowhere?” About halfway through Smellerbee’s rant, Jet started feeling like a child being scolded.

Which he isn’t. A child that is. The fire nation took that away from him, just like they clearly took it from Li. Smellerbee was right. Those sword skills didn’t come from nowhere, just like his.

“All the more reason to get him away from that ashmaker, no matter what sort of thing he’s holding over Li’s head.”

“What are you talking about.” Smellerbee snorted. Longshot raised an eyebrow.

“Think about it, why would someone like Li travel with someone like his supposed “uncle”? He has to have someone hostage or something. We need to-” Smellerbee interrupted with a disbelieving face, Longshot’s expression suffered a small pinch in his eyebrows. “Jet,” she looked to the direction of the kitchen, where Li just exited with what looked like their teas “when he gets here, I want you to look him in the eyes. Okay?” “What, why? What do his eyes have to do with anything.” “Just trust me.”

The man of the hour got to their table with a somewhat constipated expression, last time Jet saw it, the old man said some kind of proverb that only Longshot seemed to really understand.

Li muttered something about their order while depositing it, but Jet was too busy trying to look into his eyes to really say anything beyond a grunt of acknowledgement. What was so special about his eyes anyway, they were brown like any other, maybe a little pale sure but…

Golden. They were golden.

He really was his uncle. Li was a coal.

 

 

The widening of Jet’s eyes told Smellerbee he finally saw her point. Li was clearly Fire nation. Smellerbee’s current theory (come on of course she had a theory, you don’t stalk someone with looks that interesting for weeks without having a few theories) was colony child, his uncle probably too, so maybe Yu Dao or similar? Somewhere that has been occupied for generations. Enough time to have an uncle that old. His uncle escaped with him after a rare ashmaker-gaining-a-conscience moment, and they’ve been on the run since.

“He’s a coal.” Stated Jet after Li went to wait other tables.

Yeah sure that was always a possibility, whatever made Jet leave him alone. “Exactly, so we should-” “We need to get him as far away from that monster as possible.” What. “How can he even face him after what his brother did to Li’s mother.” Okay. Hold on. No. that was the opposite of what Smellerbee was going for with the whole fire heritage thing.

Don’t get her wrong she still wished death upon all of the Fire nation military, but after the whole avatar thing… well the avatar is a firebender too, right? So the fire in one’s veins isn’t necessarily the evil factor.

Longshot sighed softly while sipping his green tea, Smellerbee was feeling like she was going crazy. “No. Jet. Listen. While we all appreciate that sentiment, consider: Someone had to take him, y’know. Away. From there.” “Exactly, someone took him away from his innocent mother and look who it was, his paternal uncle. He must be planning something with Li.” “Then why would they be in Ba Sing Se! Y’know, AWAY, from the Fire nation?” “Well maybe-!”

They were interrupted by a polite cough from Longshot, who proceeded to very pointedly finish drinking his tea while casting a quite scornful look by his standards towards their untouched cups.

“Sorry Longshot…” Jet and Smellerbee both muttered in unison while picking up their tea and obediently drinking it.

Smellerbee put hers down first. “Wow, that’s amazing! I mean I hadn’t really had much tea before, but this- wow.” Jet put his down with far less enthusiasm “It’s certainly something.” clearly not happy about something made by a fire national being anything other than horrible and/or poisoned.

 

 

“Now nephew, think of this as a great opportunity!”

“An opportunity to what?! Make friends with people trying to blackmail us?”

“Exactly!” His uncle was far too jovial at the prospect of blackmail. Zuko wanted to scream.

“Prince Zuko, you need friends your age! A single tree is sure to be uprooted by storm, but a forest supports itself from the winds.” Zuko groaned. “They even share your interests, my dear nephew!”

“My interests.”

“But of course!”

“Will you elaborate on that.”

“Oh, look, your new friends need their tea refilled.” Iroh said, ever adept at not explaining himself.

“They’re NOT my friends!” Zuko grabbed his tray angrily.

“They’re not your friends yet.”

 

 

“This is some really good tea. I guess the rumours weren’t exaggerating.” Li only nodded at Smellerbee’s compliments. Clearly he doesn’t want to hear any compliments towards his captor. His stupid captor and his stupidly good tea.

Jet had an idea.

“Yeah, it’s really good, your… uncle made it, right?”

“Jet…” Smellerbee warned.

“Yeah. He makes all the tea, since apparently everyone else employed here just makes “hot leaf juice”, whatever that means.“

“Isn’t that all tea?” Jet asks. Longshot quietly scoffs. Smellerbee kept trying to turn into a combustion bender and blow Jet away with her mind.

“That’s what I said!”

Jet pushes on with his plan “Right, well, I think me and my friends would really like to meet your uncle.”

Li’s eye narrows. His brow tightens in confusion “You already met him… We all ate together on… the ferry.”

“…Right.”

Smellerbee finally decided trying to make the spirits drag Jet to the spirit world won’t work and cleared her throat pointedly “How about we get another cup of tea while this IDIOT here thinks over his words.”

Li retreated back into the kitchens just as Smellerbee stomped on Jet’s foot. Hard.

 

 

So, they were more unorganised than Zuko previously thought, that’s good. He could use that. He wasn’t exactly sure how, but he could definitely use it to… do something. That’s right.

Oh spirits, what would Azula do. He could just hear her already mocking him. She would’ve taken advantage of this yesterday. Scratch that, she wouldn’t be caught dead in this situation. She would call his working peasant work and then laugh at him… and then do something unspeakably horrible.

He had time, it was fine, he just needed to think of something between right now and delivering their tea in roughly 30 seconds, not a problem. Everything will be fine, he will push their weakness and they will pay for their tea and leave, it’s around closing time anyway, it’s all going to be just fine.

Except when he emerged from the kitchen, he saw that they have reached some sort of consensus, the two chaotic elements sitting at attention and trying to make it seem like they weren’t looking at him.

He could still salvage this. “Here are your teas. We’re closing soon, so hurry up.” The group minus the silent one looked like they finally took in just how empty the teashop had gotten, and how dark it was outside. But their bewilderment didn’t last long, as the girl spoke up. “Hey Li, we were thinking if it would be alright for us to talk. Alone. As in us and you and your uncle.”

This was it; he and his uncle were doomed.

“No.”

The wheat-mouth sputtered “What, just like that? Will you not even think about it?!”

Before Zuko could once again say no and then pray to the spirits that these “Freedom Fighters” would just let the actual fire prince and THE Dragon of the west be, his uncle decided to materialise behind him, giving Zuko a small heart attack. “We would love to have you for dinner, any friend of Li is welcome in our humble residence.”

Fratricide is brother, patricide is father, does killing your uncle have a fancy name as well? Zuko really wanted to find out, suddenly.

“That sounds lovely, we look forward to it!”

Well all-in-all it didn’t really matter what it was called, you don’t have to know the name of something to do it, do you.
