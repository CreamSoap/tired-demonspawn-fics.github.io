Fic

Leon was feeling a bit under the weather.

Not the end of the world, though still rather unusual for him. After all, if after spending 2 miserably rainy October days in rural Spain in a shirt left him with just the teensiest of sniffles, what damage could a regular spring month full of paperwork do?

Well apparently a lot. Or at least that's what Hunnigan impressed upon him after taking one look at him when he made his way to the office.

"Go home." She said, leaving no room for argument.

"It's just a little congestion, really Hunnigan, have you never heard of allergies?"

"You look worse than after that mission in Argentina."

Okay that made him pause, "Really?" sure, his nose was clogged and his joints felt a little achy but to compare it to a mission he lost half his blood on felt like a bit of a stretch to him.

"Really."

He sat down at his desk, or well, sat down at the little side table that extended from Hunnigan's desk. He was a field agent after all, this miserable month of paperwork was the longest he's ever spent in the office, "Well I feel fine."

Hunnigan rolled a little away from him in her chair, eyes tired, "Go home before I catch whatever you have."

He shot her a look, because honestly he felt okay enough. She shot back a far more scathing one, finally making Leon realise what was really going on here. While he made steady progress on his side of the paperwork(on account of his reports not requiring citations of codes and only his recollection of events) Hunnigan was still in the thick of it, so while he could afford to work while sick and sacrifice some productivity, she could not. Her concerns for catching whatever little cold he had being completely genuine.

Huh, maybe he was more out of it than he thought, they literally talked about how the deadlines were steadily approaching some two days ago.

Leon leaned back, clearing his throat a little. He supposed it didfeel a little sore, "Eh, was due for a vacation anyway." he shrugged.

Hunnigan relaxed a bit, before shooting him a more concerned look, though her tone remained neutral, "You've earned it."

Leon didn't blame her, he knew better than anyone how stubborn he could be about taking care of himself, the way he folded so quickly must have been rather worrying. Good thing he was doing this for her and not himself then.

He almost patted her shoulder as he walked past, before he thought better of it, if she leaned away from him while he was sitting several feet away, she would probably not appreciate being touched by his 'sickly' hand, "Hey don't drown under all that paperwork while I'm not here to fish you out."

She huffed a laugh, "And you better buy a NyQuil on your way home, you really do look terrible."

Leon's face soured as he grumbled a "Yea yea whatever." under his breath. He probably just needed more sleep and would be fine tomorrow.

As he parked his motorcycle in his apartment building's garage a persistent ache settled at the base of his skull, his eyes starting to burn to top it off. Okay maybe he was a little sick. He was adult enough to admit that... and the fact he was chased away from work and truly did not have anything else better to do than to lie in bed totally did not play a part in that admittance.

His phone buzzed in his pocket, heh, was Hunnigan drowning already? Or maybe there was an outbreak somewhere that needed his attention. Leon was very much prepared to take the 'being sick' thing back if that was the case. He checked the called ID only to be surprised at it not being work related at all.

He put the phone to his ear, "Hey Claire, what brings you to my phone at... I don't actually know what time it is for you right now." he coughed a little into his elbow before pressing the elevator button to get him to his floor, "What's up?"

"God, your handler wasn't kidding, you sound terrible." Claire said by a way of greeting.

Leon sighed, obviously Hunnigan would send someone to check on him, he should probably be thankful it was one of his friends and not a random agent, "Listen Claire, Hunnigan probably tried to send you because of how quickly I decided to ditch work, but I'm fine, really, she just still has a lot on her plate and clearly my presence was distracting her, so I left so she could focus."

"Uhuh." She didn't sound convinced. "How thoughtful of you."

He sighed, the tingle in his throat was starting to become more pronounced now, "But you're still heading over anyway." he knew full well her calling was nothing more than a cordial warning before she busted down his door.

"And you better be curled up in bed with a cup of tea by the time I get there." aaaand she hung up. Great. Didn't even give him time to retort he didn't have any tea.

The tingle in his throat suddenly demanded his attention as he closed his apartment door behind himself, morphing into a dry cough. Tea was just flavoured hot water anyway, right? He could make something work.

He dropped his bike keys and regular keys on the counter and started going through his cabinets. Okay sugar. Good step one. Though if it really came down to it he'd probably rather drink hot water by itself.

He paused at the back of one of his cabinets, "Huh, well wouldn't you know it. Tea." Some shitty Christmas kind he got from the blind Secret Santa at the office Christmas party last year. The higher ups said it 'improved morale' if everyone buys something generic under 20 bucks which then gets randomly gifted via drawing a number. Well hopefully it was more cinnamon than herb, with it being Christmas themed.

He put the water to boil, selecting a flavour from the tacky packaging that sounded the least offensive. The problem with herbal tea was that they never even used the herbs. The ones that got Raccoon City's Arklay mountains so popular. Not that those tasted any good, mostly just like munching grass (how they made for a semi popular ice cream flavour was still a mystery to him), but at least they actually didsomething. Why wasn't there green herb tea? Not like they were thathard to grow, right? Did they like... lose their healing properties when heated? Leon idly thought about rolling one up into a joint with a laugh which was quickly followed by a cough. Would that make him fail his drug tests? Probably not right? Not that he was seriously considering it, the thought of smoking anything was quite honestly gross to him. But ah well... thinking about it killed enough time to where the water was boiling, so it's a win in his book.

He made his tea and then sat down on the couch heavily. Wow, his joints did feel pretty stiff. And that headache slowly spread to behind his still burning eyes. Maybe he should make the best of this free day. Not that he totally couldn't power through this but well as long as he had the space to try and relax a little. Might do him some good.

He took a sip of the tea... Okay. Could be worse. Could be a lot worse. He coughed into his arm softly. Maybe could use a little sugar. He got up to get some, wow okay calm down joints you're not that far over 30, you don't have to scream like that over a little getting off the couch.

He was halfway to the kitchen when a key clicked in the lock, Claire entering without much fanfare, though immediately levelling a scathing look at him. Probably for not being in bed.

He put his hands up placatingly, "Listen, in my defence, I was only grabbing some sugar," he pointed to his coffee table, where the steaming mug of 'Christmas spell' sat, "see, I even have the tea." He was glad he was done talking because the tickle in his throat returned full force, swallowing only calling further attention to it. But he held. Claire already clearly thought he was dying, no need to cough in front of her.

Claire sighed, face softening, "I'll take what I can get, honestly I'm surprised you even had tea."

Leon laughed, "Yea, me too." Which was apparently a mistake as he couldn't hold back the cough this time, it being a rather strong one at that. Could almost be called a fit rather than a singular cough.

Claire came closer to him, putting a cold hand on his forehead, "Wow you should get your circulation checked, your hands are freezing." he joked.

Claire grimaced in return, letting her hand drop back down, "Or you're just running a fever."

Huh. Shit.

He put his own hand on his forehead and wow yea that did feel really warm. Maybe he was more sick than he'd feel comfortable admitting.

Claire walked around him, picking the mug off the table, "Okay this is a good start, but you gotta get out of your work clothes and into bed. Sweat this out. Probably just the flu. The less you fight me on this the faster I'll let you pretend you're fine." She turned to him, "And trust me. I'm not opposed to manhandling you. I will strap you to that bed if I have to." The 'I've dealt with a sick Chris and he was waymore stubborn than you about it' went unsaid but was still very loud.

"Alright, alright." Leon relented, walking to his bedroom without further complaint, standing in the middle of it awkwardly as Claire set his tea on the bedside table.

"I'm gonna check on your food situation, when I come back you better be tucked in."

"Yes ma'am." Leon responded as Claire left him to change, coughing a little after. Wow okay that headache was not going away, pulsing painfully in time with his joints. He got out of his work pants mechanically, exchanging them for a pair of sweats, then he got to his shirt, unbuttoning it with practiced ease despite the small shake to his hands, before pulling it off and pausing as a cold shiver crept up his spine. Damn, that little temperature he was running must have been higher than he thought if it has him feeling like this. He sat on the bed heavily and reached for the sleeping shirt that was thrown in the middle of it, pulling it on before dutifully tucking himself into his pleasantly cold sheets with a relieved sigh.

Big mistake on his part. Relaxing that is. Because the moment his body registered he was as snug as a bug in a rug it suddenly thought it was perfect time to have his breath hitch and then devolve into a painful coughing fit that left Leon positively wheezing and his throat sore. He washed some of that soreness down with half of the tea, only partiallyscalding himself.

Damn. It was still missing that sugar.

Claire reappeared in his door with a dripping cloth in her hand, making her way to his bedside, squatting down next to him, "Hey, feeling any better now that you're not pretending to be completely fine?"

Leon's next words sounded scratchy even to him, "No. Feel way worse actually."

Claire frowned a little, putting her hand on his forehead again, frown deepening, "I think your fever is getting worse. Listen I'll pop out for a quick sec, okay? You have nothing in your fridge. Or your medicine cabinet. Aside from enough bandages to wrap 2 mummies..."

Leon huffed a laugh, "I'll be fine, mom, I'm a big boy, I can handle you going to the shops for twenty minutes."

She laughed back, "You better be, son. As the heir to the Redfield throne you have to stay strong, lest your rightful place go to Sherry."

"Well that's not too bad then, she's sure to make a fine queen."

"Yea, you're right, I'm probably gonna poison your tea later to hasten the process."

"Finally, I shall be at peace."

She snorted, "You wish, with our luck you'll be haunting the halls of our beautiful castle for generations."

"Hehe... True." He said, closing his eyes peacefully... and then he got another coughing fit, this one only slightly better than the last, before hissing with relief at the wet cloth being placed on his forehead.

He opened his eyes just a sliver to see Claire waving at him from the door, "Well, I'll be back. Don't die. Ideally get some sleep."

"See ya." He said, coughing a little again. Fuck his throat was sore. Claire patted the doorframe with a sense of finality and a few moments later Leon heard the front door close.

Well... Sleeping didn't sound that bad right now.

Leon woke up disoriented; his throat burned with coughs and limbs were heavy and aching. Where was he? Everything felt so damn foggy. His whole body felt like it was engulfed in flames. Why was every movement so fucking difficult?

After what felt like a rather herculean effort he opened his eyes, the light from the window making his aching head pound even more. But at least it made him realise he was just in his bed.

Another round of coughs tore through him, shooting a sharp pain into his entire chest. It almost felt like...

It was back.

All at once Leon was on high alert, getting up from his bed in a movement decidedly quicker than he thought he was capable of, resulting in immediately stumbling over his own feet, vision spotty and swimming.

Fuck, how could the plaga have progressed this far without him noticing? The symptoms being this bad meant Leon had to act fast, he didn't have much time left.

He huffed a small laugh despite himself. Heh, just his goddamn luck to get infected with a mind control parasite a second time. And this time there was no convenient dentist-chair-from-hell to help him remove it, unless his neighbour redecorated from depressing beige to bioweapon scientist.

He somehow dragged himself to the door, taking a small break on the cool metal frame, pressing his face into it bonelessly. Fuuuck that felt nice.

He almost dozed off, doing his best to merge his face with the ice-cold doorframe, when another round of coughs interrupted him, making him lose his balance as the world tilted with the force of the fit, falling to his knees, the impact resonating in his whole body painfully.

But Leon was thankful for the agonising coughing fit this time, as the pain reminded him he needed to move. Shit, he was losing time, yes, all his joints felt stiff right now but judging by the way the pain radiated from his knees... he dozed in his doorframe for far too many precious minutes without realizing.

He hefted himself back up with great difficulty and despite another round of coughs and a persistent shiver that ran up his spine and into his whole body really trying their best to stop him, his mind still somewhat successfully went back to the issue of how to get rid of the immediate threat that resided in his chest.

Fuck, all his guns were in the safe right now, that was way too far for him to make it. Plus, his hands were shaking up a storm (complimenting the full-body shivers quite nicely) and felt like lead. As much as Leon believed in himself, inputting the safe combination wasn't in the cards right now. And even if it was, his physical state also meant that the faster and easier option of successfully performing the Kozachenko special on himself wasn't a feasible solution to his predicament anyway.

Each step had Leon straining to stay upright, his sense of balance completely shot, blood pounding in his ears, but eyes locked on his target despite the spotty vision. Yes, that would do. He could make it. It would work.

"Hard way it is then." he grumbled weakly, each word felt like a million knives scraping across his throat, the resulting tingle after he was done saying his piece threatening another coughing fit with just one too-deep breath. Curse his stupid penchant for talking to himself in stressful situations.

But, against all odds, he made it to the kitchen, leaning on the freezing counter heavily. His knees not collapsing by only the force of his will(which was being quickly sapped away by the soothing coolness of the faux marble against Leon's forehead and cheek) and arms(which held the counter for dear life).

With great effort and thankfully only a small coughing fit he shifted his body so one of his arms reached blindly for what he needed, before letting himself collapse on the ground, drawer handles digging uncomfortably into his back as he leaned against them.

He will need to be shirtless for this, he realised, letting the knife clutched in his hand clatter to the ground as he started fighting with his sweat-soaked t-shirt, another cold shiver running through him, making him feel the wiggling of the parasite underneath his skin.

As soon as he took it off, he regretted it, the unusually freezing temperature of his apartment hitting him all at once and somehow not at all helping the fire in his veins and head. Just like the freezing rain in Spain did fuck all for him last time.

He was getting more lightheaded, his entire body spinning despite sitting down.

He was running out of time.

Blood was roaring in his ears as he took the knife into his shaking hands again, slowly aligning it with the faded scar on his chest. If that was left after the machine was done with him, it had to be a valid entry point to cut the parasite out, right?

Well either he cuts it out or he dies trying, emphasis on the dies, wouldn't want his body to be puppeted around now, would he.

Leon took a steadying breath, trying to make the shakes in his hands at least somewhat lessen, earning him a strong coughing fit for his troubles, this one leaving him damn near choking, unable to breathe between the rattling death-like coughs.

Shit, was he already too late?

During his fit he tilted too much in one direction, his body too weak to fight against the gravity, resulting in him slumping on the freezing floor with an uncomfortable splat. But as the cool tile pressed against his sweaty forehead and he gasped greedy gulps of air, his mind unclouded a little, realising that he could now use the ground as leverage to keep the biggest of the shakes at bay.

It took way too long and way too much effort, but he managed to rearrange himself into a more optimal position, once again aligning the knife with the existing chest scar.

He faintly registered sounds coming from somewhere left of him, but his head felt like it was underwater, with a single-minded focus on his impromptu at-home surgery.

He needed it out. Out out out out OUT!

He made the first cut, the sharp pain fading into the cacophony of his full body aches, making him relax slightly, he didn't exactly have any anaesthesia on hand, so he was kinda afraid it would hurt more.

And then there were rapid approaching thuds and suddenly the knife was kicked from Leon's hands with the strength and accuracy of a football kicker scoring a goal, clattering way too far away for him to reach in his weakened state, his shallow chest wound slowly dribbling blood on the floor.

Leon used all his remaining strength to look up, meeting the wide eyes of Claire Redfield.

Right! Claire was here before. She would understand.

He tugged on her pantleg weakly, rasping out, "C-Claire... The plaga. Need. Out." while his other hand vaguely reached out to the direction of the knife. His throat burned way too much and his head was way too damn cloudy for him to be any more coherent than that.

Please, let her understand. She was still fine; she could cut it out of him.

Unless she takes too long and Leon turns.

She was still looking between him and the knife with furrowed brows, mouth open, at a loss for words. Another deathly cough tore through Leon's chest, making him tear up. If from the coughing itself or the prospect of his turned-self hurting Claire, he didn't know.

"Hurry. D-Don't have. T-Time." His teeth were chattering. Fuck it was so fucking cold here. Everything was spinning. He felt himself being manhandled so he lay on his back, his head resting on something. He opened his eyes(when did he close them?) and blinked away the tears, leaning into the blissfully cold hand now on his forehead unthinkingly.

"Fuck." Claire cursed under her breath, her eyes finally lighting up with understanding. Yes! She got it!

"Cl-aire. The plaga. Please. Hurts. Y-You have to-" He started babbling, honestly forgetting what the words he was saying meant as another cough forced itself out of him, he could feel the parasite wiggling in him again, setting his lungs and veins alight.

He must have blacked out for a bit because he was suddenly not on the floor anymore, but had one of his arms draped around Claire's neck and shoulders. He was being slapped lightly, "C'mon, c'mon don't fall asleep yet! Fuck."

"Claire. The..." he tried reaching for where he thought the knife was.

"We'll deal with it okay? But you need to go back to bed."

Leon tried to respond but coughed instead, his body shaking weakly against Claire.

"Okay one leg and then the next, okay?" She tried holding him up without touching the cut on his chest, eventually settling a hand under his armpit while the other held the hand he had around her in place.

Right. Walk. He could do that. Especially with Claire acting as a walking stick.

His head pounded painfully, vision swimming, so he closed his eyes, humming something he hoped sounded like 'I'm still awake, everything is just spinning and I need to focus on walking right now'. Claire didn't try and slap him again, so it was close enough probably.

He felt a whine escape him, they were taking too long, if he was in this much pain and this out of it, it was only a matter of time before his head would be exploding into a bunch of tentacles with scythes.

"Just a little further, man. Feel that? That's that shitty threshold I keep telling you needs sanding down." He could feel it.

They made it to his bed, Claire rolling him onto it carefully, which still made Leon's joints scream in protest and the headache behind his eyes worsen. She said a few quick words to him as she tucked his bare feet under the blanket that he tried listening to. He really did. But they mostly melded together with the heartbeat in his ears. But then she left in a hurry, which was good, she probably went to get the knife. Or something even better for the job from his weapon safe.

He almost let himself slip into unconsciousness again but then Claire returned, placing blissfully cold and wet cloths on his forehead and neck before shaking him a little, trying to get his attention. Probably about to run him through what she'll do to remove the parasite.

Leon blearily opened his eyes, noticing the curtains were drawn now. As much as his headache appreciated that, Claire really shouldn't have wasted her time on that. Time was something they had in short supply right now.

She was suddenly helping him sit up a little, holding some kind of pill in her hand, "Open up."

"Nnn..." no painkiller. They didn't have TIME for a painkiller to take effect.

"It'll make you feel better, trust me."

Leon thought about it for a while, eyes unsteadily meeting Claire's, before opening his mouth, letting Claire place the pill on his tongue and then help him wash it down with a glass of water she pulled from... somewhere. Smart smart Claire. Obviously it wouldn't have time to take effect now, for the actual removal, but it should start working about when they would be done, helping with the pain of immediate recovery.

She eased him back down, murmuring small encouragements all the while, and then pulled out another cloth, more of a wipe really, and started cleaning up his chest, the wound still adding new blood to the mess sluggishly.

Okay, she was probably cleaning it so she could see it better and resume where he left off.

At least that's what Leon assumed, before she took out a clean square of gauze, put a generous amount of antiseptic on it and pressed it on top.

No. Nononono, she didn't get it. He was still infected; she had to get it out.

His shaky hands shot up faster than his mind could register, trying to weakly push Claire's hands away, "Nnno.. T-The.." a rattling cough interrupted him, making the wet cloth on his forehead fall off, immediately making Leon miss its coolness. "P-Plaga. Out."

Claire looked at him with such a devastated expression, before easily overpowering him and continuing to hold the gauze down. Her other hand reaching for the fallen off forehead cloth, disappearing with it from Leon's limited view and then placing it back in its place with a renewed coolness that made Leon's eyes flutter closed with relief.

Claire's cool hand grabbed his wrists and one by one tucked his hands away from his chest, placing something cold and uncomfortable unto the bend of his armpit and then cupping his cheek, tracing a thumb over it with clear worry, Leon's muddled mind briefly thinking of their joke from before. About her being his mother. He almost laughed at it again, 'look how quickly her traitorous plans of his assassination fell through', but instead coughed weakly.

Her tone was light, worried, "There is no plaga Leon." He immediately bristled at that, no, this pain, this burn, he felt it underneath his skin, he didn't have much time, he needed Claire to understand, but she soothed him down again, "Leon. I need you to focus, okay? Just for a moment." Her hand left his cheek and landed on one of his wrists, lifting it easily.

"Please open your eyes, Leon. Just for a sec. Then you can sleep." She pleaded.

He tried his best, slowly moving his lead-like eyelids and finding Claire's eyes with his own. She smiled at him, moving his own hand into his field of view.

"See that Leon? No dark veins. I've seen the report, remember? You've told me what to look out for. No veins." She reached for that pressure in his armpit which he almost forgot about, revealing it to be a thermometer, "Only a, Jesus Christ," she caught herself, her tone sliding back into that reassuring calm, "one-oh-five degree fever." And now that Leon looked, really looked at his arms, she was right. Only a normal amount of veins to be seen, wearing either a cool blue colour or no colour at all and Leon felt himself fully relax, even the tightness in his chest somewhat lessening as his eyes closed with relief.

"Theeere we go, that's right. You're fine. Just regular sick. Now I'm going to clean you up and you let that paracetamol do its magic."

He hummed a little, already feeling himself slip into sleep once again.

"Heh, sweet dreams, sleeping beauty."

The next time Leon woke up he was sticky with sweat, but otherwise feeling so much better.

Well... relatively. There was still an uncomfortable pressure behind his eyes, his joints ached and his throat was sore, but at least his head felt clear.

He slowly sat up, his blanket sliding off of him, and yep, hell no, back under the blanket he goes, he is fucking cold. Cold and still shirtless.

He managed to peek at the fresh bandages on his chest though, fuck, what was he thinking? He knew how the plaga felt, the aches were completely different, not to mention the lack of dark veins or how despite feeling like he was coughing up a lung there was a distinct lack of coughing up blood. Thank god Claire showed up when she did, this way he was left with barely a scratch.

He wrapped the blanket around himself snuggly before slowly getting up. Nature called to him in more ways than one, he felt like one of those... videogame guys. With the little need meter things and green diamonds above their heads. With the meters for thirst, hunger, and needing a piss and a shower tragically low.

He opened the door to his bedroom slowly, first thing to register, aside from how dark it was outside, was that his tv was on, though with the volume turned all the way down, second thing was Claire stirring in front of it, the squeak of his bedroom door waking her up.

"Leon?" she asked sleepily, turning to him.

"Hey." His voice broke in the middle of the short word. Fuck he really needed that drink, he felt like he gargled gravel.

Glass of water then a piss and then go back to bed, the hunger could wait and well, shit, he was sick, he was allowed to be a little gross.

"Feelin' better?"

"Mhm..." he replied, making it to the kitchen and filling up a glass, immediately gulping it down. Oh yea that's the stuff...

With Claire seemingly content to just watch him and not make further conversation, Leon took a turn for the bathroom. Only once back out did he stop by the couch, thinking of how to say what he wanted to say.

"Hm?" prompted Claire, also clearly exhausted.

"Thanks... for everything."

She waved a hand, yawning, closing her eyes, "Don't mention it, I'd like to think you'd do the same if you found me delirious with fever on my kitchen floor trying to do surgery on myself."

Yea. He would.

"Thanks anyway."

He'll also have to thank Hunnigan, her shot calling was on point as always.

"Yea thank me by going to sleep and not waking up till you're healthy, how 'bout that."

"Heh... I'll go do that... Goodnight Claire."

"Goodnight Leon."
