Fic:

What a shitty fucking day.

All of them tended to be summarised like this lately, but this one. Hoo boy.

Not that it even made top 10 of Leon’s worst days ever (trademark pending), but it was just. A certain culmination, Leon supposed: on its own it would have just been the regularly scheduled bad day like any other, but as the last one in a line of very bad ones the shitty-ness of it just grew exponentially.

To start with, his first vacation in years was interrupted by the whole thing with Arias. And don’t get him wrong, he was glad New York wasn’t full of zombies and that another madman with a virus was dead, but it also meant that what was supposed to be 14 days of drinking away his sorrows from yet another shitty op that literally blew up in his face(or at least that was the provisional plan when he booked the hotel) turned into 4. The rest starting with fighting yet another horde of zombies and a giant mound of living flesh and then followed by an impromptu intervention for his alcohol problem.

Which was fine, really. It was long overdue. Rebecca, Chris and Claire (the 3 active members of the intervention)(the others that knew of it too busy to take part) were right. He needed to quit.

So that was the rest of his ‘vacation’. Not cold turkey-ing it, thank god Rebecca shut that down once he told her how much he regularly drank, but still feeling the worst of the withdrawal anyway, his body disagreeing heavily with the drastic decrease in alcohol per day, basically bound to a toilet or bed while someone held his bangs out of his face or his hand in comfort.

They changed shifts regularly he was pretty sure, but the only hand he recognised was Chris’, so sometimes he looked up expecting Rebecca, only for Claire to be there, despite him swearing it was only a minute or two since he last checked.

On one memorable occasion Hunnigan was there holding him. Leon vaguely remembered dryly remarking he was surprised she took time off work for his bullshit and that he didn’t like the devastated and angry expression she gave him back, so he turned away and pretended to fall asleep.

And right after that ‘vacation’ he got thrown back to work, dealing with the huge amount of fieldwork and paperwork Arias left behind (which he was formally spared from for at least as long as his vacation lasted on paper) (he still has to thank Hunnigan for that…) with only Professor Chambers ApprovedTM amount of alcohol in him.

But that meant the numbing static returned. It started in the back of his head and eventually by the end of some days threatened to swallow him whole, voices from his past trickling into the back of his mind like a poisoned creek.

This was one of those days. Or more so evenings/nights. He got dispatched to a newly located ex-Arias warehouse in the middle of the night and lo and behold, a whole cache of weird and experimental half lickers-half zombies who hadn’t been fed in a while.

So, Leon was once again beat up (which was not doing favours for his still healing arm and ribs), covered head to toe in blood and grime and, if the others didn’t remove every single bottle from his government issued shoebox of an apartment, perfectly in need of a drink while it was barely noon.

His hands trembled slightly as he crumpled heavily against his door. If from exhaustion, withdrawal or a combination of the two he couldn’t tell.

He sat there for god knows how long, simply staring at and feeling nothing, but still being unfortunately awake and aware, unable to move a single muscle.

This just happened sometimes, it was fine. He knew he was fully capable of movement if he needed to, it’s not like he was actually paralysed, he would just rather get shot than move a single inch right now. Happens to the best of us. And fuck, Leon was not the best.

He eventually felt a weird sort of jolt go through him, signalling the end of his floor time, his first thought being that he really needed take a shower.

After using up all the hot water, he got dressed again and…

There was.

Something wrong.

He couldn’t quite put his finger on it.

He laid down heavily on his bed (Claire insisted he get a frame even though the mattress was just fine on the ground) and stared up at the ceiling.

Huh, that’s right. He didn’t really feel any better.

Maybe he should call Claire, she was in the area, right?

No. Stupid. She already dealt with enough of his bullshit for a lifetime.

But this was.

Concerning.

Usually, the shower at least marginally did something to make him feel human again.

Huh, human again. If only all viruses had such a cut and dry reverse switch as the A-virus. He thought of Marvin. The numbing static roared, spreading to his fingers and toes. His eyes started to burn.

Others flashed before him, people he couldn’t save. All the people in Racoon City. He was supposed to protect them. Heh, at least having a long-time alcohol problem had some perks, if he wasn’t hung over from the night before, he would have probably burned with Racoon City. Just another zombie.

…Maybe that would have been for the best- No don’t go there. He had saved people. Sherry, for one. She didn’t need him anymore either, but he helped her before. Signed his life away for her and fuck he would do it again.

More unwanted memories surfaced at that… Manuela dissolving into the wind… The way Leon drove a knife into Krauser’s chest, watching the life leave his eyes. The way that same knife glinted in Luis’ back. His flask that JD took from him, how he gave him his vest back knowingly, not wanting to be a bullet-proof ganado. Adam staring into his eyes vacantly, already turned. The people of Tall Oaks. Helena’s sister, fuck he couldn’t even remember her name, how bad of a comrade was he to not even remember her sister’s name? He knew he knew it before. Why couldn’t he think of it now?

The teammates he had to shoot a month ago, the innocent voice of that rat Patricio’s daughter, asking for her daddy on the phone…

The walls were closing in around him as blood roared in his ears, each breath burning his lungs.

He stood up at some point. When did he do that? He needed to move. To do something. Anything with himself. He was pretty sure tears were streaming down his face. Fuck, when was the last time he cried? His ears were ringing.

He moved some furniture around. Maybe a change in scenery would make everything quiet again. He leaned heavily on the back a chair in the middle of his apartment. Where did he get the chair? His lungs felt on fire. Probably the kitchen, he didn’t have a lot of chairs.

The static was unbearable, he felt it in his entire body, he just needed it to end, the static, all of it, just stop for once, he was so tired of the movement. One would think having weird episodes of collapsing on the ground pseudo paralysed would help with that kind of thing but here he was. It was just one thing after another broken with him, wasn’t it.

He was in his work clothes, he belatedly noticed. Why the fuck did he change back into those after the shower, he left normal clean clothes for himself. At least he thought he did. He usually did. Now that he spared it what little thought he had he didn’t make the usual turn to the bedroom beforehand.

His shirt felt like it was choking him. Like all the monsters ever combined. The tyrant, Méndez, Jason, Arias. Fuck why did everyone have to grab him by his neck?

He was clutching his own neck. He should stop that.

Okay that made breathing a little easier.

Oh, if Sherry could see him now, what a fucking traumatized failure her ‘hero’ became. He hasn’t seen her in a while. He’s been keeping his distance, he explicitly didn’t want her there during his detox, didn’t want her to see him like that.

He would just disappoint her.

All his girls could take care of themselves now, they were fine, he was just a burden on all of them, anyway. His useless alcoholic ass. Burdening all his ‘friends’(They would leave him. They should leave him.) with his bullshit. He would relapse anyway, why even try going clean. He just didn’t want to be a burden. They all had better shit to do than to worry about him. All it would take would be just a little pressure on the trigger and-

The door to his place suddenly opened.

Not uncommon, lots of people had the key these days.

It was quiet. Leon could make out the sound of the cars outside vaguely.

The quiet was nice.

“Hey Leon, you weren’t picking up so I thought I’d just go see you myself, we have been thinki-!” Claire Redfield stood in his door, frozen.

He pulled the gun away, unbothered, his breathing relaxed, like he wasn’t struggling with it just moments ago, "Oh hey Claire, did you want anything?"

It registered way back in his mind he should probably be mortified, but strangely this was the most relaxed he's felt in a long time. The moment he put the gun under his chin the world just became blissfully quiet for the first time since, fuck, he can’t even remember.

Claire clearly made up for all the mortification necessary in the room though, freezing in her tracks and staring at him with more horror than he's ever seen on her face.

He got up from his chair, clicked Matilda’s safety back on and put it back in his holster. Huh, he didn’t even realise Matilda was the gun he grabbed. It wasn’t the one in his holster when he got home.

It was a bit poetic, he wouldn’t lie.

Where was the other gun now, anyway? Probably the bathroom.

The click of the safety made Claire flinch, breaking her out of her frozen state, her hands suddenly gripping his, eyes darting between them and his holstered gun.

"Leon what the fuck?" her voice shook.

"I just asked if you needed anything?" his face stung from drying tears.

Please let her need something. He was itching to do something with himself.

"Leon were you-" her eyes caught the sight of the note behind him, he barely remembered what was written there, or when did he write it. Did he write it? Probably. Not many other people around.

It was a safe distance away from the chair so none of his brains would get on it once he was done. Practical.

Claire's eyes widened further, it was creepy seeing her so scared.

"Claire, you're freaking me out a little here, are you okay?"

"How many guns do you have on you right now?"

"Well on me just Matilda, why? If you need me as backup I can get more fro-"

Claire let go of his hands and grabbed Matilda right out of his holster, pushing him out of the way in the same movement, striding towards the note.

Leon got between her, feeling so very far away and yet like he was miserably here and sinking through the floor, "Hey there's no need for you to read that, you have the gun, I'm fine, let's just forget all about this, what did you need me for?"

"Forget all about this?! Leon, you had a gun under your chin, you wrote a letter, you are clearly not fine." She paused, "Are you sober? We thought you were getting better."

He was, that was partly why everything has been so unbearable recently: finally recovered enough to not physically need a drink… Just really craving one.

When he told Claire about it she bought him a ‘_ days sober’ badge. It was lying on one of the counters, if he remembered correctly.

He never wrote a number on it.

"Yea I'm sober right now, really, Claire you're overreacting."

"I just found one of my best friends trying to kill himself! If anything I’m underreacting!" She shrieked, tears welling up in her eyes.

Leon was stunned from that; it finally started to dawn on him what she was almost witness to. The numb feeling of static returning at full force. This was exactly what he was trying to put a stop to. All the pity, the fussing, the being a burden.

He briefly thought of Sasha. Of what he said to him before he put a bullet in his spine with the man’s own gun. Heh. Sure, let’s add being a hypocrite to his collection of charming qualities. Right beside the hypervigilance and still-not-completely-over-a-toxic-ex-he-killed.

Claire pushed past him in his distraction and grabbed a hold of the note. She read through it quickly while Leon just stared, blood rushing in his ears. Once she was finished, she made a distressed sort of sound, crumpled the note and shoved it angrily into one of her pockets, then grabbed Leon by the forearm.

"Let's go, you can't stay here." She eyed his bland apartment like it personally offended her.

The ride went past in a blur, Leon only feeling like he was actually in the car when Claire sneaked a look at him while driving, he didn’t like how worried she seemed. No motorcycle today, he realised dimly, it seemed she wanted to drive him somewhere all along. He probably made her cancel her plans, great going idiot.

She grabbed him again (oh the car stopped now) and eventually plopped him down on a couch… somewhere.

“Now you stay here and don’t do anything stupid.”

“Mhm.” He managed, his tongue feeling like lead.

Jesus Christ he needed to get a hold of himself, he was practically catatonic here, he couldn’t have another episode in front of Claire, she already had to deal with too much of his bullshit today, least he could do was make it a little easier for her. But the proverbial genie was out of the bottle now and stuffing it back in took all he got. His limbs heavy, his head full of cotton.

Ah at least it was a slight change from his usual bouts of not moving. This one left his head positively screeching. Fun.

He managed to successfully move his arm an inch to the right, just in time to hear the door of (what he now realised was) Claire’s apartment shut, accompanied by a very specific voice.

“Claire, I’m here. What’s the situation, you said it was better to explain in pers- oh, you’re here too. Do you know what’s going on?”

Claire must have called her brother on the way here. He didn’t remember her doing that.

Despite everything, the words came easy, “Oh nothing, really Chris, everything’s fine, sorry to pull you out of work.”

Leon got up on surprisingly steady legs and took a few steps towards Chris, who filled up the entrance to the living room with his broad shoulders, and patted him on one of them light heartedly, trying to squeeze past and out and away and towards breaking his sober streak to wash all this shame down, but was stopped by one of Chris’ steel beam-like arms.

He was frowning at him slightly now, “So it’s about you.” he paused, the deep inhale he took not being lost on Leon, he was checking if he relapsed. (Heh. Not yet.) His eyes softening when he probably only smelled Leon’s shampoo. “What’s the problem.”

“Nothing, like I said, I’m just gonna go now, sorry for bothering you, tell Claire she can keep Matilda if it makes her feel better.”

A wrong thing to say, as the blocking arm didn’t move. If anything, Chris straightened up, blocking the doorway more deliberately now, a confused crease to his brows, his eyes flitting across his face, probably noticing the dried tear tracks.

“Matilda?”

Claire finally reappeared behind Leon, holding his gun in her hand, “Matilda.”

She still looked shaken, now seeming like she’s been crying and didn’t have enough time to clean up, which made the pit in Leon’s stomach double and Chris’ hold on the doorframe tighten.

Leon sighed, he was sandwiched alright.

“Chris, he tried to kill himself.”

Chris flinched above him, but Leon wasn’t really paying attention. Personally, he was trying to sink into the floor. Great, just great, now it’s going to be a whole thing. And everyone will have to deal with his shit again. The intervention was already pushing it but everyone sort of knew that one was a long time coming, even Leon.

“Well, that might be a bit of an overstatement.” He put his hands up pleadingly.

“You had a GUN under your CHIN, Leon!” Claire shouted, voice still trembling, Leon needed it to stop doing that. It scared him that anything could shake her like this.

He…

He wasn’t worth this much of a response.

“You what?” not Chris’ voice too, that was even worse. Unflappable Chris Redfield, voice shaking because of Leon’s sorry ass.

Before he could deflect, he was suddenly enveloped in a bear hug, Chris grasping onto him like he would disappear. Like he was something worth holding onto with all his strength.

That couldn’t be right.

“You should have said something. If things were getting this bad.” Chris murmured against Leon’s neck weakly, like it was his fault that Leon was such a fuckup that he was so out of it on a random Tuesday that he couldn’t even stick to his own words of suicide being the coward’s way out.

Leon finally hugged back tentatively, just sort of holding onto Chris, whereas the other was positively clutching the back of Leon’s shirt like his life depended on it, “No it’s- it’s fine. I was fine. I just…”

He just lost track of himself for a while and suddenly there was a gun under his chin. Like a normal person.

“It wasn’t… planned.”

It started to truly dawn on him what he almost did. He didn’t even fucking believe in suicide. Not like this. Sure, suicidal missions until one of them finally did him in, that was… well obviously not healthy, but fuck, what was healthy about his fucking life? At least the missions made him feel like he was doing something.

“Everything was just… too much… I guess.” He finished lamely.

Chris pulled away, still grasping his shoulders firmly. Leon had to look away from his face, he couldn’t see the Chris Redfield crying, only to be met with the face of another crying Redfield.

He decided maybe the floor was the best place to stare.

He felt like explaining.

They needed to understand it wasn’t-

That he wouldn’t normally resort to that.

“Today just. Sucked and I guess I started spiralling about my whole life and suddenly there you were and there I was in the chair and, really, I didn’t want to do that.”

“You could have called me.” Claire said softly next to him, grabbing his hand and holding it firmly.

“I didn’t wanna be a burden, you already deal with enough of my shit as is.”

“And we’ll deal with so much more. As much as it takes, Leon.” Chris’ hands tightened on his shoulders, “You’re stuck with us now.”

“Yea, we’re bonded through shared trauma, blondie.”

Leon huffed an almost laugh, “Not very blond right now.”

“You’re blond in your heart… And in your roots too once that box dye washes out.”

Leon looked back at Claire, who was still a bit sniffly, and smiled a very weak smile. “Sure.”

Leon ended up watching movies on Claire’s couch surrounded by Redfield, both taking their turns holding him in some kind of way, like they were making sure he was still there.

Like he was something to cherish.

And maybe, Leon wasn’t as much of a burden on them as he thought.

But only maybe.

It would take a lot more from both Redfields to make that maybe a certainty.
