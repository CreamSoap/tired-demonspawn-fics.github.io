Claire was just settling into her brother’s couch when her phone rang. It wasn’t an unusual thing, she was a pretty busy woman these days, after all, but she was on vacation and her work knew this, so the pool of possible callers was quite shallow at the moment.

Chris glanced her way from his utter focus at not burning a pot of pasta, “Who is it?”

Claire checked the caller ID and from all the names in the shallow pool of people who would bother her on the yearly Redfield get-together, this one was one she expected the least.

And no matter how much she was currently mad at a certain Leon Scott Kennedy, whose name and picture(a selfie of him, her and Sherry) were currently mocking her, it was also one she could never not pick up.

Especially, if she was mad at him and especially if he knew she was mad at him.

She picked up, putting the receiver to her ear almost on autopilot, dreading what kind of world ending scenario was happening now, which would motivate Leon to risk talking to her while she was still angry with him.

“Leon.”

Out of the corner of her eye, she saw her brother almost drop the wooden spoon he was holding. He also knew that the only reason you would call an angry Claire Redfield was a) you called to apologise or b) the world was ending.

What Claire didn’t expect was a secret third option that of course could only be found by Leon.

“Hey, Claire. Howzit goin?” Leon slurred, and that would be terrifying (just what kind of monster could beat up the Leon Kennedy so bad that he started slurring his words?), were it not undercut with the clear background sounds of a bar.

Claire sighed, “Leon, are you drunk?”

“Yea…” he sounded a little sheepish, “…Right. ‘m callin cuz… uhh” there was a pause, where Claire could hear a faint masculine voice, “You called to get a ride, you drank too much and are being cut off.” Before Leon cleared his throat and said, “I need a ride back home, y’see I was feelin down, so I had a walk, which turned ‘nto a run which sumhow got me here. And now ‘m bein thrown out.”

God he sounded like a kicked puppy.

“And you called me.”

“Yea, I knew you were in the city, so.”

God Claire hated him so much. He thinks he can just do this, huh? Help cover up a large government conspiracy and then barely two months later ask her for a ride while almost blackout drunk.

“Claire? You there?” She could practically hear the pout.

“Yes, I am still here, unfortunately.” she groaned and ran a hand down her face, “Where are you.”

“Uhh…” there was a bit of a shuffling noise before the voice from before said a clear address and the rough directions to get there from the freeway, then more shuffling and more of Leon’s slurred slow speech, “Yea wha he said.”

“Fine.” She said and slammed her phone shut.

She got up with a frustrated groan, “I’m going to go pick up Leon from a bar.”

“I thought you were mad at him.” Chris said from behind the kitchen island, already up to date on her and Leon’s falling out, being one of the first and only people she vented to about it.

She started grabbing her jacket, keys and wallet, “I am! Two things can be true at the same time, the fact I’m angry doesn’t mean I won’t help his government lapdog ass get home safely.” she paused, thinking about how defeated he sounded over the phone and sighed tiredly, “Scratch that, I’m going to bring him here, I don’t trust him to be alone in that shoebox he calls an apartment, plus it’s closer… Can I borrow your car?”

Chris shrugged, putting his hands up placatingly, “By all means.”

The bar was more run-down than Claire expected, but she didn’t dwell outside long.

Walking in, she was hit with the distinct smell of what a lack of “no smoking” signs along with cheap booze did to a space. There were a few tables filled with what seemed to be regulars, a pool table way in the back in a corner, a small dance floor with cracked tiles, ancient speakers that crackled with generic club music and, to bring the whole thing together, a modest bar at the center, a single barman manning it, currently fussing over a hunched over bundle of a person who was clutching a bottle of jack like it was his baby.

Bingo.

She walked closer, catching bits of their conversation.

“I’ll pay f’r it is fine.” said the blond mop of unfairly shiny hair, not letting go of the bottle.

“And I told you half an hour ago that you’ve already had way more than I’d seen a person of your size drink. Ever. And you, mister, promised me you wouldn’t drink while waiting for your ride.” said the bartender with an iron grip on the neck of the bottle and the patience of a saint.

“Claire prbly won’ even come, she’s mad at me right now…”

The bartender closed his eyes and took, what Claire assumed, was the steadying breath of a person who had to tell drunk people who wouldn’t take no for an answer ‘no’ a lot.

She finally got close enough to the bar and tiredly sighed herself, freeing the poor man, “Well Claire did come. Against her better judgement.”

The bartender looked at her with relief, then turned back to Leon, who was also looking at her, though with a more blank, almost disbelieving expression, “See Mr. Perfect Hair? Your ride is here,” in one swift motion he removed the kidnapped bottle from Leon’s arms, “and you even kept your promise not to drink till she did. Great job buddy.”

Leon swung his head back around, now smirking, propping his head up on his no longer alcohol kidnapping hand, the melancholy he displayed until a few seconds ago forgotten, “Well, since I did so great maybe I d’serve a reward?” he almost didn’t slur.

The bartender mirrored his posture, smiling sweetly, “In your dreams and in my nightmares, I don’t do one-night stands and don’t date guys married to their job.”

Leon sighed and closed his eyes, playful smile gone again, “Fair ‘nuff.” he swung his entire body towards Claire, almost toppling over from the barstool, “Okay capt’n Redfield, ‘m ready t’ go.” he mock saluted with his fingers, missing the side of his head by roughly a country mile.

Claire rolled her eyes, “You called me, not Chris, he’s captain Redfield.”

“Not like I have his number… only ‘is e-mail.” he stood up suddenly, and just as soon lost his balance and fell onto Claire, who was, fortunately for him, prepared to catch all 5 feet and 11 inches of blond the moment his sorry ass left his seat. God, he smelled like a liquor store.

He made an embarrassed sort of noise from her shoulder and tried to stand on his own, muttering a slurred “S’rry…” Well at least he had the decency to feel bad about collapsing on her.

She eventually manhandled him so he was slinging an arm around her shoulders, nodding to the bartender, “Sorry for having to deal with him.”

The bartender waved a hand, taking on the tone of someone who just finished dog sitting, “Oh don’t worry he was perfectly polite the whole time.”

“Sure he was.” Claire huffed and started the slow walk to Chris’ car.

“Calling me, what kind of idea was that, idiot, we clearly aren’t on speaking terms after what you let the government get away with.” She hissed towards the sack of potatoes with the last name Kennedy who currently contributed very little to their walk.

“Din’t ‘ave anyone else t’ call…” he replied simply.

And the thing was, Claire wasn’t angry with him for calling for a ride. Really, as one of the world’s top anti B.O.W. weapons, Claire was more than happy to make sure Leon didn’t die in a ditch after one too many drinks.

Claire was doing the world a favour right now, not him, she told the part of herself that couldn’t currently stand looking at Leon.

What she was angry about was how he had in his hand a chip that proved without a shadow of a doubt the corruption that ran through the U.S. government, and he, what? Decided that it would cause too much of a panic? Of course it would! But it was better than live in denial about how ‘perfect’ their country was.

Claire thought he would understand. That’s what hurt the most. It wasn’t as fresh as it had been, which was honestly also in large part why she was here. If this happened a week after their fight? She probably wouldn’t have even picked up the phone.

"I wanna kick your ass so bad right now. " She groaned.

Leon huffed a laugh, "Watch out Redfield, tha’ would be damagin g’vernment property, hehe."

She rolled her eyes, "Yea yea, all U.S. agents belong to the government,” then, under her breath “…asshole."

He pouted as she readjusted his arm around her shoulders, "Man, I wish I b’longed to the governmen’ like the other guys, maybe then I’d get some vacation days."

Claire frowned in confusion, "Like the other guys?"

"Mhm.” he nodded, in that way-too-deep drunk way, “If they fuck up, they jus’ get fired. If I fuck up enough times they'll jus’ kill me I think."

It was the casualness he said, or well, slurred, it with, that made Claire pause, like it was just a normal fact of life. The sky is blue, encountering a licker is always scary and the U.S. government could execute Leon Scott Kennedy any time they wanted.

"What?"

"Is all good tho, at least I can keep ‘n eye on Sherry. They said they would leave ‘er alone if I signed, but I know they lied about that, at least I can still visit ‘er sometimes. She’s so brave and amazin Claire, she’s so worth it. I love ‘er so much. She’s grown up now." he started tearing up.

What did that mean? Signed what? Claire had accidentally stumbled into connecting dots she thought unconnectable: the dots of just how did that earnest bright-eyed rookie cop become nothing but another puppet for the government. The dots that haunted her ever since their argument.

She’d given up at this point. She thought that she simply didn’t know him as well as she thought she did. After all, their major bonding moment mostly happened in one traumatic night. And while their subsequent meetings, calls and general getting-to-know-each-other only confirmed her beliefs about his character, him not handing over the evidence of governmental corruption made her question everything.

His current babbling, however, started to paint a different picture. One that more closely aligned with who she thought Leon was. And she didn’t like that one bit.

She needed to tread carefully now, who knew how far alcohol pushed Leon's boundaries of what constituted 'confidential information'.

"She really is, isn’t she, almost 20. Our baby girl." Claire smiled despite herself, she couldn’t deny how proud she was of her adopted daughter, keeping the conversation going while she thought through how to best get to the bottom of what did Leon mean by 'signed'.

Leon went silent for a moment, his expression becoming more pensive, "They still won’ leave ‘er alone Claire... they still- is all my fault, they know if they let ‘er go they won’ have leverage on me ‘n I’m- I’m too good at the shit they put me through, ‘cuz if ’m not then what would they do to ‘er y’know? But then they want to keep ‘er longer so I stay ‘n so it goes, over ‘n over, round ‘n round ‘n it’s a whole fucked up freaky circle y’know?" the tears that welled up in his eyes at the first mention of how amazing Sherry was now spilled freely over, no longer the tears of a proud dad but of a sad drunk, "Fuck, if I jus knew better I could ‘ave found a better way, I should ‘ave stalled till you came back fr’m Europe with Chris, is all my fault we’re all tangled up in this red tape bullshit now."

He was blaming himself for Sherry’s situation. Why? She was confined due to Wesker still being out there somewhere. Claire hated all the tests she was subject to as much as humanly possible, but she understood that Sherry unfortunately always had to be protected.

During his weepy rant, they got to her brother’s car, finally, and to Leon’s credit, he managed to sit himself down and at least put the seatbelt around himself, even if Claire had to click it in place.

“Leon, Sherry has to be under protection, people like Wesker are still out there. I don’t like it either but it’s the best we have. The B.S.A.A. doesn’t have the jurisdiction to take her.” she said as she took her own spot in the driver’s seat and started the car.

Leon shook his head, “I know tha’, that’s not. I jus. Now I can’t even help you expose a conspiracy or they’ll hurt ‘er.” He put his head in his hands, “Evrythin’s spinnin…”

Was Leon being blackmailed? That was- They wouldn’t- But it would explain so much- But surely, if Sherry was used as a bargaining chip it would have to be going on since Raccoon city. Was that what Leon meant about stalling and waiting for her and Chris? Surely not, right? That would be. Beyond fucked up.

Claire was still reeling from this revelation that she was honestly a little surprised when she successfully parked in front of Chris’. She cringed at the danger she just posed on the road. It was barely a ten-minute drive on empty roads, but still. Didn’t exactly feel good on the soul.

She looked to her right where Leon still had his head in his hands, but by the even-ness of his breathing it looked like he dozed off.

“Leon?”

“Mhm?” he blearily shot his head up, still very drunk.

Ah well. Biting the bullet and taking the direct approach. Leon didn’t have the mental faculties right now to lie to her, anyway. Or at least lie to her competently. She felt a bit bad about taking advantage of that, but it was for the greater good.

“Did you sign your life over so Sherry would be safe?”

He blinked at her a few times, then withdrew away from her, curling into himself, grumbling something that vaguely sounded like ‘classified’ under his breath, not meeting her eyes. More horror went through Claire’s veins. He was being blackmailed. All this time.

Oh god that’s. Horrible. She thought he was like Chris, choosing the fight. Turns out he was coerced this whole time.

Leon gulped audibly next to her, in that way drunk people did when they’re about to throw up and suddenly Claire was very aware she wasn’t sitting in her own car. She got Leon unbuckled and out just in time for him to start dry heaving into one of the parking lot flowerpots.

“Mmm fine… I don… throw up I jus.. spit ‘n heave…” he mumbled out as Claire held his hair back from his face, her anger with him slipping away almost completely.

“Sure you don’t.”

“No, really I-…your hands are cold… ‘s nice… I don’ throw up when drunk… I jus get a lil sick, I spit a lot ‘n then ‘m cool…” He spat one last time and then straightened up a little, “This isn’ my place. Yours?”

“No, it’s Chris’, I’m staying over.”

“Mkay.”

She slung his arm over her shoulders again once he looked more certain on his feet(and that he wouldn’t throw up on her) and they made the trek to her brother’s front door, through the hall and finally depositing Leon on the living room couch. Where he fell asleep basically as soon as he hit the faux leather.

And Claire just. Stared at him. What else was she supposed to do? One of her most trusted friends, who she was mad at for acting like nothing more than a government pawn just drunkenly revealed he was a government pawn in the worst way imaginable: the metaphor way more literal as chess players usually owned their pieces.

She got reminded of Jason, briefly. How he was forced to commit atrocities. Would Leon do that? Claire knew he broke protocol semi frequently, but what if he didn’t get wiggle room one day? What would he do?

Fuck, her friend was actually owned by the government. Or as close to it as he legally could be, probably. Or illegally as well, with all the corruption.

Chris came up behind her, she almost didn’t hear him over her own swirling thoughts, but her brother was just truly shit at stealth. Plus, he probably wasn’t trying to be stealthy right now, just quiet enough to not wake the sleeping man on his couch.

“So that’s the famous Agent Kennedy huh? I thought he’d be taller.” he joked in a whisper, blanket and bucket in hand, before tensing up, seeing how freaked out Claire was.

“What’s wrong? Did something happen?”

Claire just turned to him and then motioned for him to follow her into the guestroom, which was more just her room than anything else. Only other person who sometimes occupied it was Jill as far as she knew.

Chris took a moment to drape the blanket he brought over Leon and put the bucket by his head, before joining her, gently closing the door behind him.

“What’s going on? He didn’t do anything did he?”

Claire was pacing a circle into the carpeted floor, “Well, yes, but not to me, not tonight. He- Fuck Chris this is- I don’t know what to do with this.”

Chris watched her with concern, crossing his arms “Why don’t you start from the beginning. So, I can see how I can help.”

Claire gripped her hair loosely, “Well that’s the thing Chris, I don’t think we can do anything anymore. Not even you, the B.S.A.A. doesn’t have the jurisdiction on this. TerraSave doesn’t have even a shred of a chance.”

“I still don’t know what’s the problem.”

“They own him, Chris. That’s why- That’s why he didn’t give me that chip, why he’s been so distant about everything.”

“Well… all U.S. agents are technically government property.”

“See I thought that’s what he meant too, but he- after Raccoon city, when we had to separate so I could find you?” Chris nodded, “They made him sign… something, so Sherry would be safe. He- he didn’t let much slip, but. They own him, Chris. They’re blackmailing him with Sherry.”

Chris looked pale, his mouth slightly open.

“All this time I thought he was like you, Chris, that he chose to fight. Oh god was that what he meant when he told me he couldn’t follow my path in Harvardville? I thought that, yea, he joined to have the clearance to keep an eye on Sherry, but I thought that was just one of the reasons. He- I-”

Her spiral got interrupted by two steady hands on her shoulders, she met Chris’ eyes, “Listen Claire, I may not know him myself, but from everything you’ve told me and some of his reports I’ve read, I think he would have ended up on this path anyway. Blackmail or no blackmail.”

Claire sighed. Chris was right, every time Leon would talk about eradicating bioterrorism there was this determination in his eyes, one you couldn’t fake. It’s just that right now it felt like her mental image of Leon shifted a few feet to the left and she was left doubting every little detail in it.

Chris continued, “As for the blackmail, I can try to pull a few strings, and maybe… No if they realise we’re onto them that would jeopardize Sherry and him.” he slipped into his captain tone, quickly muttering different scenarios of how he could help and looking more and more dejected with each failed one, Claire along with him.

Sherry wasn’t a minor anymore, that could be a good playing card, if only her borderline imprisonment didn’t have a very valid reason on paper in the form of a certain Albert Wesker.

The mention of him made Chris clench his fists tightly.

They both slowly ran out of ideas on how to help, the room going quiet, not that they had much to go off of in the first place, Leon didn’t exactly tell her any more sensitive information, just the bombshell itself.

Chris glanced around, then sighed a very final sounding sigh, “It’s late. We should just. Talk to him tomorrow. Hell, corner him till he spills if we have to. Worst comes to worst and we truly can’t help him right now, I think just showing he has someone in his corner can go a long way. He… He seems pretty lonely.”

Claire unfortunately had to agree. She’d gotten a glimpse of his phone contacts once or twice and the only person that wasn’t outright work related was her, as far as she could tell.

“Yea, you’re right. Tomorrow we settle this.”

Chris offered her a small smile, “Redfield style.”

She mirrored it, “Redfield style.”
