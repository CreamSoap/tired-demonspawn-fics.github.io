chapter 1: Self sacrificial idiot

Start notes:

here we go ig lmao

Soulmates are... interesting, is what the common consensus is.

Not many other ways you can describe a weird unexplainable mythical force that makes you feel someone else's pain. Not to even mention that that someone else is apparently a person you are 'fated' to be with, in one way or another.

The actual pain itself is also... interesting. Because you definitely feel it on your own body, and yet you simply know which pain is your own and which is your soulmate's. As well as your soulmate's pain not exactly hurting. It sure felt like normal pain. But it also didn't. It was weirdly divorced from an actual physical feeling. Aside from being startled by it, it didn't even make you wince or slow down, it was simply there. A connection.

Chris knew his soulmate has had a hard life. From as early as he can remember feeling them, they didn't just have the average burn of a scraped knee or maybe a broken bone or two, but belts, fists. He was relieved when it stopped around when Chris was 15, finally feeling only the normal pains that came with everyday life.

He also found out from Claire that his soulmate was probably a guy. Or at least they were born a guy, when at 19 he still hasn't felt his soulmate have a period, which, according to Claire(whose soulmate wasborn a girl) was something you could feel.

The next big discovery about what Chris' soulmate might be up to came some 4 months before his life got changed forever by that hellish mansion: when he started waking up feeling that his soulmate was sore to high hell and also at specific times during the day it felt like they were getting their ass beat.

It was all very familiar. Softened thuds of training mats, that one persistent burn that was clearly from a stray bullet casing hitting exposed skin and a crick in the neck from staying up late studying. Some kind of basic training, or maybe police academy, with the amount of studying.

Chris wasn't one to get excited over soulmate related things, he was more of the opinion that if the universe was so almighty in assigning him someone, then it would also decide when they would meet. And if that when was never, so what? Plenty of people never met their soulmate. That being said he did feel a little giddy that maybe as soon as what, 6 months? He could be meeting his soulmate as the new rookie at the RPD. Not getting his hopes up, of course, just idle daydreaming.

Of course, life wasn't that simple. And after fighting the living dead and getting involved in taking down a really fucking evil pharmaceutical company, Chris' soulmate was one of the last things on his mind.

He of course noted how the police academy theory was wrong, his new theory being some kind of special ops, the aggressive change in training right after Raccoon City only supporting that. It was one thing to prepare cadets for whatever, but Racoon City changed every level of the military as well as the world at large.

And after that the spec ops theory got all but confirmed by the pains, stabs and bruises that could only be from mission deployments.

But again, that was all in the background for Chris.

At least until it literally stared him in the face.

The report came to his desk like any other news of B.O.W.s being discovered. This time it was from USSTRATCOM, a report on how the president's daughter was kidnaped by a bioweapon worshiping cult in Spain. Or at least that was the cliff note version that was under less clearances.

Chris was a bit surprised to learn there was just one agent on scene, but then he learned it was Claire's friend Leon and he couldn't help but smile; Claire did share how he got recruited by the U.S. government after Racoon City. Another good soul in the fight. Though from a slightly different angle than himself.

First was the dry report of the incident in varying amounts of detail. Chris huffed a little laugh, Leon wrote down damn near every scraped knee and broken nail Ashley got. Of course it made sense, she wasthe abductee Leon was there to rescue, after all. Not to mention her being infected.

Chris shuddered at the thought. She was so young. Just a college student. Not that he was crazy older than her, but still.

But well, the overly catalogued injuries were pretty funny in comparison to how Leon 'described' his own injuries. Especially with the knowledge everyone was safe and sound now. Chris could relate, unless he was forcefully dragged to medical(aka his injuries were documented by other people) he also tended to not really mention any hurts of his own.

He finished the annotative part of the report without giving it much thought, mostly thinking about what details could he share with Claire that weren't considered classified. Because if she was to be believed, Leon would brag about a lot but never his actual accomplishments. And well, someone had to brag for him.

Then came the technical details of the bioweapon, 'Las Plagas' it was called. A parasitic organism operating on a kind of hive mentality, with a dominant variant controlling the subservient variants.

He read on in relative calm, well as relative as it can be with the discovery of a new way the world was in danger, at least until he read the symptoms of infection. And then read them again. That couldn't be right. Sure, he knew from the report of events what Ashley went through but... this was perfectly describing what his soulmate- what Chrishad felt some 2 months ago... exactly when the incident happened.

Chris' heart sat heavy in his chest. Was his soulmate... dead? Lost to a bioweapon before Chris could even meet them? The universe keeping their connection alive through the sick and twisted loophole that technically their body was still walking?

He read on mechanically, getting to the removal section.

'With correct application of targeted radiation [technical details in attachment LSN_035] a plaga can be killed inside the host before reaching maturity, saving the host. The operation is noted to be extremely painful and leaves behind outer scarring.'

The attached diagram made Chris partially sigh in relief. That kind of pain. That placement. He felt that. His soulmate at least attempted to remove the plaga. They could be fine.

But who had the access to the removal technology? Only people Leon mentioned and the ex-Umbrella employee was dead and Chris sure as hell knew through Leon's meticulous cataloguing of her injuries that Ashley Graham didn't go through the kind of pain Chris knew his soulmate experienced. Plus, she was way too young for how long their connection has been alive.

But... there was another person on that mission, wasn't there.

He read the dry factual report again. Beartraps, pitchforks, molotovs, an extensive knife fight between Leon and Jack Krauser... It all eerily matched to what his soulmate... But... Leon didn't mention anywhere that he was...

"That self-sacrificial idiot..." Chris found himself mumbling. Of course he wouldn't think to mention his own life being at risk. He barely mentioned anything that happened to him. Positive or negative. It was all passive voice bullshit. 'The path was blocked by B.O.W.s', 'The door opened with an emblem found near the lake'.

Chris almost didn't even realise he dialled Claire's number till he heard her voice on the other side of the receiver.

"Hey Chrissy, wassup?"

"Claire you can contact Kennedy, right?" he tried and failed to sound normal.

"Leon? Uh, yea, why?" she sounded suspicious, like someone about to cover for a best friend by giving fake alibi about where they were at the time of murder.

"I just wanna talk to him, uhh I think it's about time we met, right? You always wanted me to meet him." Fuck 'letting the universe figure it out', he knew who it was. He could meet them- him. This was his universal sign. Or at least he was very determined to take it as such.

"Well. Yea, but- Chris, you sound weird, are you okay? Why do you really want to meet Leon?"

He probably should have led with his suspicion/damn near certainty; Claire was always way more into who his soulmate is than he ever was. (Just his mind you, she felt the same 'let the universe handle it' way as him about her own soulmate.) But he was still reeling.

Has Leon told anyone? This was the full report. Chris knew that for a fact. It was rare but it happened. The discovery of a brand-new, not T-virus related type of bioweapon taking precedent over USSTRATCOM being huffy about sharing their secrets. And not a single mention?

"Claire I- ...I think he might be my soulmate."

"...What?! Okay wait. Back up. Why now. What- How? You never even met!" she sounded both exited, cautious and disbelieving.

"Do you know how we thought my soulmate was in a helicopter crash or something in October?"

"U-huh."

"I got a report today about a new type of bioweapon. It was written by Leon. The- The timelines match, the symptoms match-"

"The symptoms? You mean he is-"

"Was infected. There is a cure, if caught early enough. But- I don't think he told anyone."

"Told anyone what? That he was infected?" she sounded horrified.

"Yea. This is one of the rare full reports and- There were other infected and he described their symptoms in detail, but- oh my god, it would make sense, wouldn't it? I did think my soulmate was in the police academy, then spec ops. Hell, you said he had a bandaged shoulder at the end of Raccoon, which would go with how I suspected they got shot around that time."

They were both silent for a while. Stewing in that revelation.

Chris did share, however unwillingly and after a lot of nagging, his soulmate-related theories whenever they came up and they both must have realised just how much they matched.

"I think..." Claire eventually broke their silence, "I think this might be a better conversation to have in person."

"Yea." Chris' throat felt dry.

He blearily jotted down when and where would they meet before hanging up.

Fucking hell.

chapter 2: A pretty successful first meeting, all things considered

Start notes:

This one picks up right where the other left off, the others are more disjointed bits and pieces ig, random timeframes I thought would be cool, but these 2 are kinda the introduction to the au and like I had to make them meet somehow.

It's also... massive lmao. Like the others are all like ~1,6-2k but this one is like 5k.

Rest in peace Peter Cullen my goat forever and ever, transformers was a great part of my journey both as an artist and as a person and the voice he provided for Optimus was a big part of the way these shows impacted me.

They somehow managed to plan a weekend get together on a miraculouslyshort notice given all their busy careers, but it did help that Leon was apparently still on forced medical leave after Spain. (Good. He needed it. Though it being this long was... troubling.)

The siblings had a plan: Chris and Leon would get introduced, get to organically know each other a little and then Claire would 'accidentally' hit Chris with something, or maybe she'd punch him? Point was she would cause him pain in clear view of Leon who would have to connect the dots, figure it out, and then they would... do whatever soulmates who have found each other did? And Claire would have a word with Leon about not telling anyone he was infected.

Him and Chris probably wouldn't start dating right away, admittedly Chris was not the type of person to simply fall for people on first meeting, even if they were apparently his universe-assigned 'the one'.

But it would be a start. Get introduced. See where it goes... Never mention the convoluted scheme and set of fortunate and unfortunate events that got them there.

Because they couldn't just corner the man with something like this. Sure, the Redfields were both like 99% sure it was him, as every one of Leon's injuries they could find out about matched with pain Chris remembered feeling, but there was still that 1% of doubt. This was as much a confirmation of their connection for Chris as it would (hopefully) be for Leon.

They picked one of their favourite restaurants for the initial meetup and dinner and then the plan was to continue to the Redfield family home (which the siblings didn't sell out of sentimentality more than anything, but they have since come to appreciate having one fixed address that didn't change when their work needed them to move) for a movie night.

The Redfields sat in a corner booth, quickly dismissing the waitress that they were still waiting for a third person to join them, and then they went over the possible ways Claire was to inflict pain upon Chris throughout the evening. Normal sibling talk.

"See I think it should be at multiple points. Like a thing or two here and leave the more 'accidental' ones for later. As much as I love embarrassing you we both love this spot too much to make a scene."

Chris hummed and nodded, "Yea I was thinking about that too, one instance can be chocked up to coincidence, multiple ones make you think about it. But you have to be careful, we're walking a fine line here, one hit too many and he could become suspicious that we're doing it on purpose."

Claire huffed a laugh, rolling her eyes a little, "Who do you think you're talking to here? I'm a professional. I've been hitting you around people I thought might be your soulmate for years now."

Chris sputtered, "You have?!" how has he not noticed? Years? She had to be joking, right?

Claire changed the subject, eyes lighting up as she started waving to someone across the restaurant, "There he is! Look suave or whatever, this is my future brother-in-law you're meeting here."

Chris blushed a little, muttering "Bit presumptuous here..." under his breath.

Finally, their table was approached by, admittedly, the most gorgeous man Chris had ever seen: sharp and intelligent blue eyes partially hidden under dirty blond bangs, strong face contours contrasted by a contradictory softness that still clung to his cheeks and jaw and although covered by a shirt and a leather jacket, Chris could tell he was muscular. Though clearly in a more lithe, gymnast-y way than his own brute-strength focused bulk.

Claire got up to hug him, a gesture he very stiffly reciprocated, and although his smile seemed relaxed, it didn't escape Chris' notice how Leon's eyes kept flicking around the room.

Chris felt his heart melt a little with sympathy, this kind of post-mission hypervigilance was familiar to him too, weeks where you barely register being alive while subconsciously on the lookout for the next B.O.W. that's hiding around the corner.

The beautiful blue eyes turned to him, finally, breaking him out of his thoughts with the intensity of their gaze, giving him a (alsofamiliar) paranoid onceover before slightly softening, "So, you must be the infamous Chris Redfield. Glad to finally meet you in person, you're basically all Claire talks about."

The last comment made Claire elbow him, with an annoyed, "Hey! I talk about other things too!" which devolved into them bantering as they each took a seat; a conversation Chris was too preoccupied to listen to as he was processing that he felt it. The elbow to Leon's arm.

It really was him.

Chris exchanged a meaningful look with Claire when she briefly glanced his way, only getting a knowing smirk in return. Oh, wow she really was good at the 'incidental' soulmate hitting.

Chris felt warmth in his chest, this was his soulmate! Wow he felt like a little kid again: so giddy at the prospect of falling in love with a universe-assigned special someone.

Their snipping quieted down enough for Chris to have an opening to talk, "Hey, pleasure's all mine." He leaned in a little, "You should have heard her after we found each other post Raccoon, wouldn't shut up about you too."

Leon laughed warmly at that, picking up the menu the server left with them and looking through it idly, and wow that was the sound Chris would get to hear for most of his life? Thank you, universe.

Claire turned to him now, annoyed, "Well excuse me, I just got out of a zombie apocalypse and we trauma bonded!"

Leon waved a hand, "Oh just admit it, Redfield, you simply adore me."

Honestly? Chris could see himself doing that. Not just yet, but Leon definitely made a great first impression. Of course, it was helped by the heaps of good words Claire put in for him through the years, but still. Hell, maybe there was even a grain of truth to all those accounts of feeling a spark right away when meeting your soulmate, though Chris would still consider himself sceptical of that.

Claire scoffed, smiling, "Sure I do, blondie."

"So... What do you do for work probably not an icebreaker I can use, right?" Chris said with a small laugh, changing the subject.

Leon returned it, though his body was rigid, twitchy, losing that small bit of relaxation it held until now, "Heh, yea. Unless you want to hear my wonderous stories of redacted a bunch of redacted, that is. Pretty sure that question can be levelled against you, though?"

Chris straightened up a little, "Kinda. You heard of the BSAA?"

Leon's eyebrow shot up a little, "Bits and pieces, hasn't been around for long, right? So you also in the business of doing redacted, huh?"

"Heh, more or less. Though ours has a different flavour to it I guess."

"Yea I can imagine. So heh, work talk is off the table!" Leon visibly brightened up and relaxed tapping the table with his hands, "That just leaves- um. Everything else." he faltered in the middle, clearly struggling to think of a conversation topic.

Claire sighed, "God, you guys are hopeless. And married to your jobs." she paused before lighting up, "Ah right! So, you ended up buying that bike we were checking out together?"

Leon blanked for a moment, "Hm?"

"That blue uhh... Honda? Or was it a Harley I can't remember right now."

His tone and expression remained blank, other than his eyebrows going the slightest bit up in recollection, "Oh right, that one. Honda. The Harley you liked was next to it."

"That one, yea! You went for that test drive?"

Leon looked away, eyes a bit unseeing, fingers tapping against the table idly, "No. I uh. Got called for the Spain mission before I could and I guess it just hasn't crossed my mind since."

Claire and Chris exchanged a meaningful glance, which told Chris that the way Leon was acting was not normal for him and that it was Chris' turn to carry the conversation now.

"You a bike guy too Leon?"

That broke Leon out of his daze, expression brightening a bit, looking at the two of them again instead of being lost in his head, "Oh yea, unlike you. Haven't been kissed by the feeling of wind in your hair and just you and the road, or so I hear." he turned to Claire with a light teasing smirk, "You sure you're related to this guy Claire?"

Chris laughed, "No see, I get the wind and the freedom, but I like my wind and freedom while surrounded by a big metal crumple zone."

Claire rolled her eyes, smiling as well, "Or maybe you're just boring."

Chris put his hand out, gesturing into Claire's personal space a bit, turning to her with an incredulous look, "Boring for not wanting for my body to be a crumple zone if someone rams me on the road?"

Claire slapped his hand away with a soft sting, "Just don't get rammed, duh."

Leon flinched in Chris' periphery.

Chris sighed, putting his hands up, "Whatever! We always have this argument." He put his hands down again and met Leon's eyes, which were observing him now... a little intensely too... weird. "Point is I've always preferred cars."

"Which is why you joined the Airforce and you naming jet models off the top of your head used to be my bedtime story."

He turned back to Claire again, "Hey flying things were not part of this conversation until right now."

"It is also why our only mode of transport for a while was yourmotorcycle and we also got matching motorcycle jackets."

Right. His dark past... So what? He was too broke to afford anything else, too sentimental to sell off the family home to not be that broke, the bike was compact, Claire liked it and it did make him seem the cool older brother, so what if it gave him a little heart attack whenever it would slightly wobble.

"Well, it's your motorcycle now. So technically. There is no such thing as my motorcycle... and the jackets were just cool."

Claire sighed, leaning her head on her propped-up arm, "Yea... they were. Shame yours went with Raccoon City." Claire's bike along with it. It was why his own fell to her.

Leon got up, "I'm gonna go take a piss, if the server comes, I want a vodka coke and whatever food you guys think is good. Don't kill each other while I'm gone, heh."

Claire called after him, "No promises!" then watched as he walked off, before she looked at Chris with a serious expression, "He's doing bad, Chris, a full conversation about bikes and he didn't interrupt to defend their honour against your incorrect opinion. I'm sorry to say but we might have to move the timeline a bit on the wedding."

Who said anything about a wedding?

Chris could see Claire's point for himself though, "Yea he still seems a bit... high strung. We're sure he hasn't seen any action since October?"

"You tell me. He hasn't mentioned anything."

Chris frowned, he hasn't felt anything other than healing pains and the occasional stubbed toe. Though there were a fair bit more of those as of late. As if he was more uncoordinated. "I don't think so."

But that would mean he's still doing this bad even after 3 months. The siblings both grimaced, the unspoken implication between them, the tough atmosphere only broken by the waitress stopping by their table and taking their order, which they quickly scrambled to give with what they both hoped were polite enough smiles that didn't at all show they were in the middle of an unspoken horrible realisation that their friend/soulmate respectively was doing bad with very likely nobody there to support him. Once she left though, Claire let out a deep sigh and put her hands on the table resolutely.

"New mission objective, make Leon completely relax. You!" she pointed at Chris, "Forget that he's your soulmate. Or don't. Whatever mindset will make you cheer him up better." She paused, thinking, before putting her hands together, brows furrowing, "I get you can't tell me the classified details or whatever, but any topics I should avoid that will probably make him think about Spain?"

Right, good call. He probably would have come up with it on his own but the actual verbal acknowledgement of Leon being Chris' soulmate managed to resonate around his head rather loudly.

"Bugs, parasites and mind control to start."

"Jesus. Mind control?"

Chris couldn't help the paranoid look around the restaurant, nodding slightly, before moving on, "The obvious I guess, Spain, the military." He grunted in frustration rubbing his face with his hand.

"What?"

"There was a... well he wasn't a complete civilian but- point is there was an ally who died, but the report didn't detail how, so-"

"-So if it was something specific, we'll find out when we step on thatparticular landmine. Great."

"Yea."

Claire sighed and gestured with her hands defeatedly, "Anything else?"

Chris grimaced, "Probably best not to bring up his other past traumas?" It hasn't escaped Chris' notice how Leon needed to use the bathroom right as Raccoon City got mentioned.

Claire cringed, "Yea, that was totally my bad, fuck, he's been there for a while too, he's totally composing himself in there, I've seen it before, he'll be doing bad, then excuse himself for a few minutes and then act like he's fine and then either he really is fine and just needed a breather, or he breaks down 2 hours later, shit."

Shit indeed, maybe Chris should go check on him-

Claire straightened, expression shifting to something more neutral very suddenly, "Well the CGI was strange to watch, sure, but you can't lie to me, the only reason you didn't watch Beast Wars was because they no longer transformed into planes."

Ah, shit, Leon was returning, time to act normal.

He schooled his expression to the best of his ability to what he hoped was appropriate for the conversation him and Claire were totallyhaving for the duration of Leon's absence, "Well maybe I've decided I'm too old for kids' shows." He blurted out a response quickly, then grimaced at how untrue it was. But he'd need to stick to this point for a bit to keep up the ruse that they were totally not talking about Leon the whole time he was away.

Claire scoffed, "Too old for transformers, too old for motorcycles, you hearing this Leon?" she tilted a little from their booth to the approaching Leon, who, true to Claire's word, looked more composed.

Leon chuckled and sat back down, "Heh, I think he's just embarrassed about you reading him like a book, so he's making up stupid excuses."

Claire nodded with a smug smile, "My thoughts exactly. Not to mention," she paused, locking eyes with Chris who felt himself stiffen. She wouldn't.

Leon leaned in conspiratorially with an interested smile of his own, "Not to mention what?"

"Not to mention the Starscream figurine he has in his room which he prayed to before all his big tests and job applications. He still might, actually."

An F-15, I even got the special collector's edition figurine, so when he's in his alt mode he's actually a rather accurate model-" Chris cut himself off, fuck, he almost started rambling about how he used that model to better understand his Airforce manuals and how based on the shape of the cockpit it would be one of the older one seater variants. Great, he didn't even need Claire here to show Leon how embarrassing he could be. "Anyway, that doesn't matter, point is, you never seen transformers?" he finally met Leon's eyes, who was leaning in, head propped up against one hand, his gaze weirdly hot against Chris' skin, making him look away again, this time to Claire, who was glancing between the two of them and looking extremely pleased with herself.

Leon leaned back relaxedly at the question, "'Fraid not, my folks thought the robots not having soulmates was 'depraved' or something."

Now even Claire joined, "Well that's stupid, the episodes where Sparkplug and Spike teach them about soulmates are the coolest ones."

Chris huffed a laugh, "Yea, made you forget you were basically watching a glorified toy ad, since the writers actually explored the wider lore for once."

"Yea, like honestly you gotta appreciate it. They could have just went with the easier 'Cybertronians also have soulmates', but opted not to."

Leon hummed thoughtfully, "And Cyberwhatchamacallits are...?"

"The transformers." Replied the siblings in unison.

Leon nodded, "Right, okay."

The server came with their order then, handing all of them their drinks and food, Leon idly commenting, "Let's see if I'll ever trust your tastes again, Redfield." He paused, "I would specify which one, but I don't know which one of you actually ordered for me."

Claire waved a hand, getting a knife and a fork with the other, "Ah it was a mutual agreement, we've been going here for years, so we have our favourites and recommendations."

"might as well get you started on your missed education."

So what if he did, huh? Also the 3d animation they chose was just plain old creepy. Claire swore that by episode 4 you barely even notice it aside from select moments, but Chris had his doubts.

Leon huffed a soft laugh, "Well if Claire doesn't mind, sure, time to see what I missed, I guess."

"Very.") and Leon shared his preference for teen romance books in his childhood as he downed the rest of his second drink. ("What can I say, I'm a romantic at heart.") (the way Leon stared at him while saying that made Chris hide behind his own drink...)

They invited him, after all, at worst they should have split the bill. When the waitress walked away with Leon's credit card they turned to each other in puzzlement and wonder at how did he manage that. But truth be told, Chris was privately just a little bit glad. Not that he didn't make enough to live comfortably, but decidedly being a part of a private anti bioterror militia was only paying as much as the sponsoring pharmaceutical federation felt was necessary.
